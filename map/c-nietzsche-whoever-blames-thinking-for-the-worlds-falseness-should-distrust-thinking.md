The main clause of the aphorism's second sentence, from »Wer aber« to »Misstrauen zu
lernen«. The statement drops the »aber« that joins the sentence to the first
([c-nietzsche-erroneousness-of-the-world-the-surest-thing](../../c/nietzsche-erroneousness-of-the-world-the-surest-thing/)), the colons, the resumptive »ein Solcher« before the main verb, and the two questions after the last colon, »hätte es uns
nicht bisher den allergrössten Schabernack gespielt? und welche Bürgschaft dafür gäbe es,
dass es nicht fortführe, zu thun, was es immer gethan hat?«, which are in the passage and in
no statement; it keeps »mindestens«, »endlich«, the subjunctive »hätte« as "would have", and
the parenthesis on the »advocatus dei«, with »jeder bewusste oder unbewusste«. The sentence
names no one: it speaks of whoever makes »unser Denken selbst, also "den Geist"« responsible
for the falseness of the world and takes this world, »sammt Raum, Zeit, Gestalt, Bewegung,
als falsch erschlossen«, and says of such a one what he would have occasion to learn. The
comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) sets it, since 2026-10-21, in its row on what in the world
of appearance is the subject's own, beside Kant's space as »die subjective Bedingung der
Sinnlichkeit« ([c-kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility](../../c/kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility/)) and Schopenhauer's
forms of the intellect ([c-schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-and-forms-of-the-intellect-two-expressions-for-one-thing](../../c/schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-and-forms-of-the-intellect-two-expressions-for-one-thing/)),
as the nearest the map's Nietzsche passages come to that row's question; that those are the
views the sentence speaks of is the map's reading and not the text's, which names neither. No
attack link is set: the statement draws a consequence for whoever holds that view, that he
would have occasion to distrust thinking, and denies nothing that either statement says of
space; a reader who holds that it denies one of them may dispute the absence.
