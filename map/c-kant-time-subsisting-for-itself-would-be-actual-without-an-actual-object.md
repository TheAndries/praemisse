The »denn« clause of the first sentence of [KrV A 32–33 / B 49](../../p/krv-a32-33-b49-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things/), conclusion (a)
on time (AA 3:59.22–23), the ground of the sentence's first limb, that time is not something that
would subsist for itself ([c-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things](../../c/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things/)). The text's »im ersten Fall«, "in the first case", points to
that limb, and the statement restates the case as its antecedent, "were time something that
subsisted for itself", so that it reads alone; the restatement is the map's resolution of the
phrase. The subjunctives »würde ... sein« and »wäre« are kept as "would"; »dennoch«,
"nevertheless", is kept; »ohne wirklichen Gegenstand« "without an actual object". The clause is a
conditional whose antecedent Kant denies in the main clause it grounds, so it shares and denies
nothing, and no `shares_premise_with` or attack is set. It is a premise of the map's argument
[a-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things](../../a/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things/), where its statement is `f -> r` and the premise that nothing is actual without an actual
object, `not r`, the denial of the consequence, is the map's and not the text's, said there. The
ground for space at A 26 / B 42, »Denn weder absolute, noch relative Bestimmungen können
vor dem Dasein der Dinge ... angeschaut werden« ([c-kant-determinations-not-intuited-a-priori](../../c/kant-determinations-not-intuited-a-priori/)), covers both limbs of that
sentence, property and relation; that sentence has no limb on space subsisting for itself, so this
clause has no parallel there. The claim sits in no row of the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), naming neither the thing in itself nor what
divides it from appearance, and is named there under *What the three say*.
