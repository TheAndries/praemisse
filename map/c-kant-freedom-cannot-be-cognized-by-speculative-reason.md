The concessive clause of the sentence at B xxviii (AA 3:17.28–18.06), »Ob ich nun gleich
...«, before the »so kann ich mir doch« that is
[c-kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds](../../c/kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds/).
»Von der letzteren Seite betrachtet« refers to the sentence before, where the will was
taken »als einem Dinge an sich selbst angehörig« (3:17.26–27), and the statement spells
the side out as that of the thing in itself; the text says »the latter side«. Three steps
are in the sentence and kept: the soul so considered cannot be cognized by speculative
reason; »mithin« neither can freedom as a property of a being with effects in the world of
sense; and the reason, »darum weil«, that cognizing it would require cognizing a being as
determined in its existence and yet not in time, impossible for want of an intuition. The
negative claim is about cognizing (»erkennen«), not thinking; the same distinction as at
B xxvi ([c-kant-things-in-themselves-thinkable-not-knowable](../../c/kant-things-in-themselves-thinkable-not-knowable/)),
here applied to freedom.
