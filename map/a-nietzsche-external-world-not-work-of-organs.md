The argument is the second half of JGB 15, which the text itself calls »eine gründliche reductio
ad absurdum«: »Aber dann wäre ja unser Leib, als ein Stück dieser Aussenwelt, das Werk unsrer
Organe! Aber dann wären ja unsre Organe selbst - das Werk unsrer Organe! Dies ist, wie mir
scheint, eine gründliche reductio ad absurdum: gesetzt, dass der Begriff causa sui etwas
gründlich Absurdes ist. Folglich ist die Aussenwelt nicht das Werk unsrer Organe -?«

What the form captures and what it leaves out. Premises 1 and 2 are the two »dann« sentences,
each a conditional whose antecedent is the consequent of the step before, the first grounded in
the text (»als ein Stück dieser Aussenwelt«), the second not. Premise 3 is the map's bridge: the
text does not say that organs which are the work of our organs are a causa sui, nor that what
is absurd does not obtain; it calls the chain a reductio ad absurdum and names the supposition
under which it is one, and the map reads the two together as the conditional that, if the
concept causa sui is thoroughly absurd, our organs are not their own work. A reader may dispute
that reading, or hold that the text's absurdity is not the causa sui but something else in the
chain. The conclusion is the conditional the text's »gesetzt« makes it, from the supposition to
the denial; its antecedent is the supposition atom for atom, and the form proves no more than
the claim states. The text's closing »-?« is kept in the conclusion claim's statement and on its passage,
and the form does not represent it: a truth table has no mark for a conclusion put as a question.
The form does not cover the first sentence of the aphorism, on the sense organs
([c-nietzsche-sense-organs-not-appearances-in-idealist-sense](../../c/nietzsche-sense-organs-not-appearances-in-idealist-sense/)),
which gives no premise to the chain, nor the »Andere« whose view the chain answers, who are
not named. JGB 21 asserts in Nietzsche's own voice that the causa sui is a self-contradiction
([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/));
the argument does not take it as a premise, since JGB 15 supposes and does not assert, and
the conclusion would then lose the supposition the text gives it.
