original: "Das Naturgesetz, daß alles, was geschieht, eine Ursache habe, daß die Causalität dieser Ursache, d. i. die Handlung, da sie in der Zeit vorhergeht und in Betracht einer Wirkung, die da entstanden, selbst nicht immer gewesen sein kann, sondern geschehen sein muß, auch ihre Ursache unter den Erscheinungen habe, dadurch sie bestimmt wird, und daß folglich alle Begebenheiten in einer Naturordnung empirisch bestimmt sind: dieses Gesetz, durch welches Erscheinungen allererst eine Natur ausmachen und Gegenstände einer Erfahrung abgeben können, ist ein Verstandesgesetz, von welchem es unter keinem Vorwande erlaubt ist abzugehen oder irgend eine Erscheinung davon auszunehmen; weil man sie sonst außerhalb aller möglichen Erfahrung setzen, dadurch aber von allen Gegenständen möglicher Erfahrung unterscheiden, und sie zum bloßen Gedankendinge und einem Hirngespinst machen würde."
translation: "The law of nature, that everything that happens has a cause, that the causality of this cause, that is, the action, since it precedes in time and, in regard to an effect that has arisen, cannot itself always have been but must have happened, also has its cause among the appearances, through which it is determined, and that consequently all events are empirically determined in an order of nature: this law, through which appearances first constitute a nature and can yield objects of an experience, is a law of the understanding, from which it is permitted under no pretext to depart or to except any appearance from it; because otherwise one would set it outside all possible experience, but thereby distinguish it from all objects of possible experience, and make it into a mere thought-entity and a figment of the brain."

The second paragraph, by the 1781 edition's indentation, of the subsection headed Erläuterung der kosmologischen Idee einer Freiheit in Verbindung mit der allgemeinen Naturnothwendigkeit, the explanation of the cosmological idea of a freedom in connection with the universal necessity of nature, whose heading stands at AA 3:368.14–16 and in no passage, from »Das Naturgesetz« to »machen würde« (AA 3:368.22–34): one sentence counted by full stop, its colon after »bestimmt sind« joining the subject, the law of nature with its three »daß« clauses, to the predicate that resumes it with »dieses Gesetz«, and its semicolon joining the »weil« clause to the main clause it grounds. The count of paragraphs follows the 1781 edition, which sets the sentence off as a paragraph with an indent and a space above it (A 542, on the scan); korpora's rows put no blank row between 3:368.21 and 3:368.22, so by its rows the sentence closes the subsection's first block, the two sentences of method at 3:368.17–21 before it. The paragraph before it (3:368.17–21), two sentences on Kant's having first sketched the outline of the resolution and now setting out the moments of its decision, is in no passage; the paragraph after it (3:369.01–12), three sentences by the map's count, that the scruple about a chain of causes allowing no absolute totality has already been removed in the general assessment of the antinomy, that yielding to the deception of transcendental realism leaves neither nature nor freedom, and the question whether the same event that is a mere effect of nature can be regarded as an effect from freedom, is [a passage since 2026-11-09](../../p/krv-a543-b571-the-question-whether-the-same-event-can-be-regarded-as-an-effect-of-nature-and-as-an-effect-from-freedom/); the fourth paragraph (3:369.13–23) is [the passage held since 2026-11-06](../../p/krv-a543-544-b571-572-among-the-causes-in-appearance-nothing-can-begin-a-series-of-itself/). The sentence straddles the page break of the 1781 edition inside the word »möglicher«: A 542 ends with the syllable mög- and the catchword »licher« beneath it, and A 543 begins with the word's tail (3:368.32–33), both read on the scans, so the span is A 542–543 / B 570–571; the A pages are the 1781 edition's, checked against the page-by-page transcription of its scan on de.wikisource.org (Seite:Kant_Critik_der_reinen_Vernunft_542.png and 543.png) and on the two scans themselves, which show the heading, the indent, the catchword and the text as the transcription has it. The B pages are given by the concordance from A 532 / B 560, the first page of section III, and are unverified: the korpora transcription marks no B page breaks, and the AA line is the authoritative locator. The 1781 text, as the transcription and the scans read it, differs from the Akademie text in orthography (»Caussalität«, »seyn« twice, »kan«, »bestimt« twice, »ausserhalb«, »blossen«) and in punctuation: a colon after »Naturgesetz« where the Akademie text has a comma, no comma after »bestimmt wird« (»bestimt wird und daß folglich«), a comma after »bestimmt sind« where the Akademie text has the colon (»bestimt sind, dieses Gesetz«), a comma after »abzugehen« (»abzugehen, oder irgend«), and no comma after »unterscheiden« (»unterscheiden und sie zum«); no word differs. The map's text is the Akademie-Ausgabe's. The wikisource transcription marks »Handlung«, »entstanden«, »geschehen« and »Natur« bold, its mark for emphasis, and the scan shows the four words set off from the text around them; nothing in the span is set in another face on korpora, which the map records and does not carry into the translation. The main clause is asserted in the indicative, »ist ein Verstandesgesetz, von welchem es unter keinem Vorwande erlaubt ist«; the three »daß« clauses state the content of the law in the Konjunktiv of such clauses, »habe« twice, and in the indicative, »bestimmt sind«, with »da sie in der Zeit vorhergeht ...« a causal clause inside the second giving why the causality must itself have happened, and »folglich« inside the third drawing the law's third clause from its first two, a step inside the law's own statement which the map does not formalize; the »weil« clause grounds the main clause's prohibition, that it is permitted under no pretext to depart from the law or to except any appearance from it, its »sie« being the appearance excepted, and has »würde« as its verb, three infinitives, »setzen«, »unterscheiden« and »machen«, hanging from it. The sentence is one claim, the whole of it: [c-kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance](../../c/kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance/). »Naturgesetz« is "law of nature" and »Verstandesgesetz« "law of the understanding", where A 532–533's »Verstandesgrundsatz« is "principle of the understanding"; »Naturordnung« "order of nature"; »allererst« "first", as at A 532–533 (»die allererst in der Zeit entspringt«, "that first arises in time"); »abgeben« "yield"; »Vorwand« "pretext"; »abzugehen« "to depart"; »auszunehmen« "to except"; »Gedankending« "thought-entity", a word in no other passage in the map; »Hirngespinst« "figment of the brain", the project's rendering of a compound whose second half is a web spun; »d. i.« "that is", as at A 538 / B 566; »in Betracht« "in regard to"; »die da entstanden« "that has arisen", the »da« of the relative construction not rendered by a word and the elided »ist« supplied, »entstanden« "arisen" as at A 532–533; »Begebenheiten« "events", as at A 533–534, A 536, A 539–540 and A 543. Three pronouns are resolved: »da sie in der Zeit vorhergeht« and »dadurch sie bestimmt wird« bind to »die Causalität dieser Ursache, d. i. die Handlung«, both nouns feminine and set in apposition, so "it" is the causality, that is, the action; »man sie sonst ... setzen« binds to »irgend eine Erscheinung«, the appearance excepted, the predicate »zum bloßen Gedankendinge und einem Hirngespinst« being singular where the plural »Erscheinungen« would take a plural, so "it" is that appearance, and the sentence's own words say what excepting it would do. »von welchem ... davon« doubles the relative with an adverb, "from which ... from it", kept. »dadurch sie bestimmt wird« has »dadurch«, singular, bind to »ihre Ursache«: the causality is determined through that cause, where the English "through which", standing after "the appearances", could be read of them; the map reads it of the cause and says so here. »dadurch aber« is "but thereby", the »aber« kept; »setzen« "set", the map's rendering of the verb elsewhere. The subsection's first paragraph (3:368.17–21) and everything after its sixth (3:371.15 ff., A 547 / B 575 onward) are not in the map; its sixth (3:369.36–371.14), on the principle of causality, the assumption of natural causes with a faculty that is only intelligible, and man as appearance of the world of sense, natural cause and intelligible object, is in the map since 2026-11-12; its fifth (3:369.24–35), two questions and the explication of a term, is in the map since 2026-11-10; its third (3:369.01–12) is in the map since 2026-11-09.
