The third sentence of the passage. The statement drops »hineinmischen« and »wie wir es immer
getrieben haben«, which repeat; the list of ten is kept whole, since »die Freiheit« and »den
Grund« are in it and the aphorism turns on the first. The sentence reads as Nietzsche's ground for the
one before it ([c-nietzsche-in-the-in-itself-nothing-of-causal-connections](../../c/nietzsche-in-the-in-itself-nothing-of-causal-connections/)): the in-itself
has none of these because we made them; the text joins the two sentences by no connective, and
the map formalizes no argument. Where Kant's B xxvii restricts the principle of causality to
objects of experience and Schopenhauer's Anhang makes the law of causality a function of the
intellect, this sentence makes causes something we »erdichtet haben« and read »als "an sich"« into things; the
comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) sets the three side by side and names what each says of
causality's standing.
