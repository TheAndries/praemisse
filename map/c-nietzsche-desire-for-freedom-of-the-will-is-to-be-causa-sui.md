The second sentence of aphorism 21, the first of the passage, whole; the statement drops only »nämlich« and adds an "and" to the list »Gott, Welt, Vorfahren, Zufall, Gesellschaft«; the »nämlich« ties the sentence to the one before it, »Die causa sui ist der
beste Selbst-Widerspruch ...« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)),
as saying what the pride of man's entangling itself with that nonsense consists in: an
explanation of the sentence before, not a step drawn from it, so the map sets no
`depends_on` between the two and formalizes no argument. »In jenem metaphysischen
Superlativ-Verstande« is kept in the statement, with the relative clause on the
half-educated and the Münchhausen clause: the sentence says what the desire for freedom of
the will *in that sense* is, and names no thinker who holds it, only the half-educated in whose heads it prevails. No passage in the map
identifies that sense with Kant's freedom »als Eigenschaft eines Wesens, dem ich Wirkungen
in der Sinnenwelt zuschreibe« (B xxviii,
[c-kant-freedom-cannot-be-cognized-by-speculative-reason](../../c/kant-freedom-cannot-be-cognized-by-speculative-reason/)) or with the antinomy's
active being that would begin its effects in the world of sense »von selbst«
([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)), and the map does not identify
them; nor, since 2026-10-27, with the idea reason creates for itself at A 533 of »einer Spontaneität, die von selbst anheben könne zu handeln, ohne daß eine andere Ursache vorangeschickt werden dürfe« ([c-kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself](../../c/kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself/)), the nearest Kant sentence in the map to a cause of itself, which Kant asserts as reason's idea and not in its content, and which this sentence names no more than it names the others. No attack link is set on Kant's claim that freedom can be thought, its representation
containing »wenigstens keinen Widerspruch«
([c-kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds](../../c/kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds/)): a denial of it would
need this sentence joined to the one before it, that the causa sui is a self-contradiction,
and the map's convention requires the denying words inside one statement; and Kant's
sentence stands under »wenn unsere kritische Unterscheidung ... statt hat« and speaks of a
representation, this one of a desire. A reader who holds that JGB 21 denies what B xxviii
states may dispute the absence. The comparison [x-the-will](../../x/the-will/) sets
the sentence in its row on freedom, beside Kant's and Schopenhauer's, since 2026-10-20.
