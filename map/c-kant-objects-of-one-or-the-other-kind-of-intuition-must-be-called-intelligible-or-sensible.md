The second and last sentence of Kant's footnote at B 312
([the note](../../p/krv-b312-footnote/)), whose mark stands after »intelligibilis,« in the first
sentence of the paragraph on the moderns' use of the terms *mundus sensibilis* and
*intelligibilis* (AA 3:212.29); the note is absent from the 1781 pages, as the passage says, and
the map holds it as the second edition's. The sentence is a prescription on naming, »müssen ...
heißen«, and the statement keeps it as one; its opening »aber«, which sets it against the
sentence before, is dropped from the statement and named here. The sentence before it, in the
passage and in no claim, prescribes that one must not use the expression of an intellectual world in place of the expression the marked sentence names, and gives the ground, asserted, »denn intellectuell oder
sensitiv sind nur die Erkenntnisse«: cognitions are intellectual or sensitive, objects
intelligible or sensible, the note's rule, as the map reads the two sentences together.
»Anschauungsart« is "kind of intuition"; »die Objecte also« "the objects therefore";
»unerachtet« "notwithstanding"; »Härte des Lauts« "harshness of the sound".

What the sentence says is what the division of objects into sensible and intelligible goes by:
the kind of intuition of which alone an object can be an object. The chapter's sentence at
B 311, that the division of objects into phenomena and noumena cannot be admitted in a positive
sense, »obgleich Begriffe allerdings die Eintheilung in sinnliche und intellectuelle zulassen«
([c-kant-division-into-phenomena-and-noumena-not-admissible-in-a-positive-sense](../../c/kant-division-into-phenomena-and-noumena-not-admissible-in-a-positive-sense/)),
divides concepts into sensible and intellectual, as the note divides cognitions; whether B 311's »Begriffe« fall under the note's »Erkenntnisse« no passage in the map says, and the map sets no link. B 307's noumenon in the negative sense, a thing »so fern es nicht Object unserer sinnlichen Anschauung ist«, with the positive sense that assumes an intellectual intuition ([c-kant-noumenon-negative-only](../../c/kant-noumenon-negative-only/)), names one thing by the kind of intuition of which it is or is not an object, the note's own criterion, and B xxviii names the two kinds of representation, the sensible and the intellectual ([c-kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds](../../c/kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds/)); that the one criterion is the other is the map's, drawn by no connective, with no link. The word »intelligibel« stands in the map's Kant passages also at A 538 / B 566, »Ich
nenne dasjenige an einem Gegenstande der Sinne, was selbst nicht Erscheinung ist, intelligibel«
([c-kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance](../../c/kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance/)),
of something in an object of the senses and not of an object by its kind of intuition, and in
the sentence the note is attached to, of an object thought as in itself merely intelligible
([c-kant-object-taken-as-noumenon-when-thought-as-in-itself-merely-intelligible](../../c/kant-object-taken-as-noumenon-when-thought-as-in-itself-merely-intelligible/));
the map does not say how the uses relate, and sets no link. In the other thinkers' statements
in the map the word names a character or a freedom and never objects by their kind of intuition:
Schopenhauer's § 55, Kant's intelligible and empirical character, which he keeps
([c-schopenhauer-kant-first-showed-coexistence-of-necessity-and-freedom-by-intelligible-and-empirical-character](../../c/schopenhauer-kant-first-showed-coexistence-of-necessity-and-freedom-by-intelligible-and-empirical-character/)),
JGB 36's »intelligiblen Charakter« and GD »Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 8's »"intelligible
Freiheit"« ([c-nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself](../../c/nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself/)); no
statement in the map of either thinker prescribes or refuses a name for objects by the kind of
intuition, and a prescription, by the map's convention, shares and denies nothing, so that no
link is set. Each absence is open to dispute.

Set on the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), in the row on the
division of the world into appearance and thing in itself, since 2026-11-02, for what it says the
division of objects into sensible and intelligible goes by, the row's first question.
