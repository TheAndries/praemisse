Eine Realität aber, die keines von diesen Beiden wäre, sondern ein Objekt an sich (zu welcher auch Kants Ding an sich ihm leider unter den Händen ausgeartet ist), ist ein erträumtes Unding und dessen Annahme ein Irrlicht in der Philosophie."
translation: "That from which abstraction is here made is, as will later, I hope, be certain to everyone, always only the will, which alone constitutes the other side of the world: for this is, as on the one side through and through representation, so on the other through and through will. A reality, however, that would be neither of these two but an object in itself (into which Kant's thing in itself too has unfortunately degenerated under his hands), is a dreamt-up absurdity, and the assumption of it a will-o'-the-wisp in philosophy."
