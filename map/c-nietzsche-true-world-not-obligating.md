GD, »Wie die ›wahre Welt‹ endlich zur Fabel wurde«, stage 4: »Und als unerreicht auch
unbekannt. Folglich auch nicht tröstend, erlösend, verpflichtend«. The »Folglich« is the
step the map formalizes as
[a-nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate](../../a/nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate/),
whose form reaches only »verpflichtend«, since the ground the stage gives, »wozu könnte
uns etwas Unbekanntes verpflichten?«, speaks of obligation alone; this claim is
restricted to that, and the stage's full sentence with its three predicates is
[c-nietzsche-true-world-not-consoling-redeeming-obligating](../../c/nietzsche-true-world-not-consoling-redeeming-obligating/),
which no argument in the map reaches. The framing as the positivist stage's position is
explained on the argument. Stage 5 draws the next step from this one,
[c-nietzsche-true-world-idea-refuted](../../c/nietzsche-true-world-idea-refuted/).
