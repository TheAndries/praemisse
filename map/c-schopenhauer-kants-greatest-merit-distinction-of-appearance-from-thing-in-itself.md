The first sentence of the appendix. It does three things in one sentence, and the statement
keeps all three: it names the distinction as Kant's, calls it his greatest merit, and gives
the ground on which it rests, »auf Grund der Nachweisung, daß zwischen den Dingen und uns
immer noch der Intellekt steht«, from which the »weshalb« clause draws that the things cannot
be cognized as they may be in themselves. The ground is stated as Kant's showing, not as a
thesis Schopenhauer puts forward on his own account here; what he makes of it on his own
account is § 24 ([c-schopenhauer-forms-in-consciousness-independent-of-objects](../../c/schopenhauer-forms-in-consciousness-independent-of-objects/)) and the
sentence of this appendix on the law of causality as a function of the intellect
([c-schopenhauer-causality-sensation-and-space-are-subjective](../../c/schopenhauer-causality-sensation-and-space-are-subjective/)). The »weshalb« clause
says what Kant's A 42 / B 59 says, that what objects may be in themselves »bleibt uns
gänzlich unbekannt« ([c-kant-things-in-themselves-unknown](../../c/kant-things-in-themselves-unknown/)), and the link
`shares_premise_with` is set between the two: »auf Grund der Nachweisung, daß ...« is the
factive construction of »Er zeigte, daß ...« in nominal form, to call it a Nachweisung is to
say it was shown, and the map reads the one as it reads the other
([c-schopenhauer-kant-showed-laws-of-experience-not-applicable-to-derive-existence](../../c/schopenhauer-kant-showed-laws-of-experience-not-applicable-to-derive-existence/)),
so both statements assert that things cannot be cognized as they may be in themselves; the
comparison names the pair under that premise. A reader who holds that a sentence praising a
doctrine for its ground does not assert the ground may dispute the link. The same sentence stands at the head of the comparison's row on what can be said of
the thing in itself ([x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/)). The attack this appendix makes on Kant's
derivation ([c-schopenhauer-kant-derivation-cannot-reach-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-kant-derivation-cannot-reach-thing-in-itself/)) comes twenty
pages later and leaves this merit standing, as
[c-schopenhauer-only-the-derivation-is-faulty](../../c/schopenhauer-only-the-derivation-is-faulty/) says.
