The fourth and last sentence of Kant's footnote at B xxvi
([the note](../../p/krv-b-xxvi-footnote/)). The statement resolves »Dieses Mehrere« as the
something more the sentence before requires to ascribe objective validity to a concept that
is a possible thought, the »einem solchen Begriffe« of that sentence
([c-kant-objective-validity-requires-more-than-logical-possibility](../../c/kant-objective-validity-requires-more-than-logical-possibility/)),
drops the »aber« that joins the two sentences, and keeps »kann auch«, "can also", so that
the sentence asserts a possibility and not that the more does lie in practical sources;
»eben« is rendered "simply" and noted on the passage. The sentence names no
practical source and says nothing of morality, freedom or the will. The preface returns to
the practical at B xxviii–xxx, where morality needs only »daß Freiheit sich nur nicht
selbst widerspreche«
([c-kant-same-action-in-another-relation](../../c/kant-same-action-in-another-relation/))
and God, freedom and immortality cannot even be assumed »zum Behuf des nothwendigen
praktischen Gebrauchs meiner Vernunft« unless speculative reason is deprived of its
pretension to extravagant insights
([c-kant-practical-assumption-requires-removing-speculative-pretension](../../c/kant-practical-assumption-requires-removing-speculative-pretension/));
that the note's »praktischen« points there is the map's reading, no connective joins the
note to those sentences, and no link is set. No Schopenhauer or Nietzsche statement in the
map speaks of a practical source of a concept's validity, so no shared premise and no
attack link is set, and a reader may dispute the absence.
