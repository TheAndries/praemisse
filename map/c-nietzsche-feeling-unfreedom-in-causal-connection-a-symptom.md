The second sentence of the aphorism's close, after the first dash; »fast immer« is kept, and so are »schon« and »bereits«; the statement keeps the colon and drops the dash before »die Person verräth sich«. »"Causal-Verknüpfung"« and »"psychologischer
Nothwendigkeit"« carry Nietzsche's quotation marks, kept in the statement. The sentence
judges the thinker who feels so and says nothing of whether the will is free or of what
there is in the »"An-sich"«; the sentence before it, in the same passage, says what the
unfree will is ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)), and the comparison [x-the-will](../../x/the-will/) sets
this one in its row on freedom for the word »Unfreiheit«, as a sentence about the feeling
of unfreedom and not about the will. The three sentences that follow it in the passage, on
the two sides from which »die "Unfreiheit des Willens"« is taken as a problem, »wenn ich
recht beobachtet habe«, are in the passage and in no claim.
