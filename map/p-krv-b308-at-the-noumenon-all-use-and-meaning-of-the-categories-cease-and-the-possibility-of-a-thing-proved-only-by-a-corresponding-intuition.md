Nun kann aber die Möglichkeit eines Dinges niemals bloß aus dem Nichtwidersprechen eines Begriffs desselben, sondern nur dadurch, daß man diesen durch eine ihm correspondirende Anschauung belegt, bewiesen werden. Wenn wir also die Kategorien auf Gegenstände, die nicht als Erscheinungen betrachtet werden, anwenden wollten, so müßten wir eine andere Anschauung als die sinnliche zum Grunde legen, und alsdann wäre der Gegenstand ein Noumenon in positiver Bedeutung."
translation: "Where this unity of time cannot be met with, hence in the case of the noumenon, there the whole use, indeed even all meaning of the categories ceases entirely; for even the possibility of the things that are to correspond to the categories is not at all to be seen, for which reason I may appeal only to what I adduced right at the beginning of the general remark to the previous chapter. Now, however, the possibility of a thing can never be proved merely from the non-contradiction of a concept of it, but only by one's supporting this concept with an intuition corresponding to it. If, therefore, we wanted to apply the categories to objects that are not regarded as appearances, we would have to take as ground an intuition other than the sensible, and then the object would be a noumenon in the positive sense."

Three sentences, counted by full stop, from »Wo diese Zeiteinheit« to »in positiver Bedeutung«
(AA 3:210.12–24), in the chapter *Von dem Grunde der Unterscheidung aller Gegenstände überhaupt in
Phaenomena und Noumena*: the first 3:210.12–18, its semicolon after »auf« joining two halves of one
sentence by the map's count; the second 3:210.18–21; the third 3:210.21–24. The span begins on the
line on which [KrV B 307–308](../../p/krv-b307/) ends, after »bestimmen können«, and ends on the line
on which [KrV B 308–309](../../p/krv-b308-309/) begins, before »Da nun«; the two end lines are shared
with those passages. With it the chapter's text from 3:209.32 to 3:210.34 is in the map without a gap,
and the stretch named in no passage on 2026-11-02 is held; of the chapter's text from 3:209.32 on,
only 3:210.35–211.14, between B 308–309 and the paragraph at A 254–255 / B 310–311, is in no passage.

**Second-edition text, as the two passages it joins.** Wikisource's page-by-page transcription of
the 1781 scan was read for A 249–254, the pages of the first edition's text at this place of the
chapter, and none of the three sentences stands on them, nor the words *Zeiteinheit*,

**Translation.** »diese Zeiteinheit« "this unity of time": the »diese« points back to the sentence
before, the last of B 307–308, where the categories have meaning »nur in Beziehung auf die Einheit der
Anschauungen in Raum und Zeit«; that this sentence says »Zeiteinheit« where the sentence before names
space and time is the text's, and the map renders the word and does not supply space. »angetroffen
werden kann« "can be met with"; »mithin« "hence"; »beim Noumenon« "in the case of the noumenon",
»bei« rendered by the phrase; »da« "there"; »der ganze Gebrauch« "the whole use"; »ja selbst«
"indeed even"; »Bedeutung« "meaning" here, as the map renders the same word in the sentence before,
»in Beziehung auf die Einheit der Anschauungen in Raum und Zeit Bedeutung haben«, "have meaning",
where it renders »Bedeutung« "sense" in B 307's »in positiver Bedeutung«, in B 308–309's »nur in
negativer Bedeutung« and in this span's third sentence; the two renderings of one word, of the
categories' meaning and of a sense of a term, are the map's and named here; »völlig auf« "ceases
entirely"; »denn selbst« "for even"; »die den Kategorien entsprechen sollen« "that are to correspond
to the categories", »sollen« kept as "are to"; »läßt sich gar nicht einsehen« "is not at all to be
seen", »einsehen« "see" as at B 307 and B 310; »weshalb« "for which reason"; »nur ... berufen darf«
"may appeal only to"; »anführte« "adduced"; »in der allgemeinen Anmerkung zum vorigen Hauptstücke
gleich zu Anfang« "right at the beginning of the general remark to the previous chapter", »Hauptstück«
"chapter", as the map calls the chapter this span stands in; »Nun kann aber« "Now, however, ... can";
»niemals bloß aus« "never merely from"; »dem Nichtwidersprechen eines Begriffs desselben« "the
non-contradiction of a concept of it"; »sondern nur dadurch, daß man diesen ... belegt« "but only by
one's supporting this concept", »man« "one's", »diesen« resolved as the concept, the resolution the
map's, »belegt« "supporting"; »eine ihm correspondirende Anschauung« "an intuition corresponding to it";
»bewiesen werden« "be proved"; »Wenn wir also ... anwenden wollten« "If, therefore, we wanted to
apply"; »die nicht als Erscheinungen betrachtet werden« "that are not regarded as appearances"; »so müßten wir ... zum Grunde legen« "we would have to take as ground", where A 540's »den Erscheinungen in Gedanken zum Grunde legen«, with its dative, is "lay ... under the appearances" on that passage; »eine andere Anschauung als
die sinnliche« "an intuition other than the sensible"; »alsdann« "then"; »wäre« "would be".

**What is held and what is not.** The first sentence is a claim set in the row on what can be said
of the thing in itself of the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) since
2026-11-03 ([c-kant-at-the-noumenon-all-use-and-even-all-meaning-of-the-categories-cease](../../c/kant-at-the-noumenon-all-use-and-even-all-meaning-of-the-categories-cease/)), for what its main clause and its »denn« clause say ceases at the
noumenon and cannot be seen; its »weshalb« clause, Kant's appeal to what he adduced at the beginning
of the general remark to the previous chapter, the *Allgemeine Anmerkung zum System der Grundsätze* (AA 3:198.13 ff.; B 288–294 by the standard concordance, not verified), in no passage in the map, is in the passage, dropped from the claim's statement and
named there. The second sentence is a claim set in no row of that page, since it names neither the
thing in itself nor a noumenon, and named there under *What the three say*
([c-kant-possibility-of-a-thing-never-proved-from-the-non-contradiction-of-its-concept-but-only-by-a-corresponding-intuition](../../c/kant-possibility-of-a-thing-never-proved-from-the-non-contradiction-of-its-concept-but-only-by-a-corresponding-intuition/)). The third is a claim set in the row on what can be said
([c-kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense](../../c/kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense/)), for what its last clause says the object would be, under the supposition, and what
that would require; the text draws it from the two before it by »also«, and the claim names both in
`depends_on`. The first two sentences are asserted outright; the third is a conditional whose
antecedent the text does not assert, »wollten ... müßten ... wäre«. The sentence after the span,
B 308–309's, opens »Da nun eine solche, nämlich die intellectuelle Anschauung«, naming the third
sentence's »eine andere Anschauung als die sinnliche« as the intellectual, and draws from it that the
use of the categories can in no way reach beyond the limit of the objects of experience, a clause in
that passage and in the statement of no claim, said on the third sentence's claim.
