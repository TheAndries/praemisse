From the chapter *Von dem Grunde der Unterscheidung aller Gegenstände überhaupt in Phaenomena
und Noumena*: the whole of Kant's own footnote, two sentences, whose mark stands after
»intelligibilis,« in the first sentence of
[the paragraph on the moderns' use of the terms mundus sensibilis and intelligibilis](../../p/krv-a256-257-b312-313-the-moderns-use-of-mundus-sensibilis-and-intelligibilis-and-the-question-answered-in-the-negative/)
(AA 3:212.29, A 256 / B 312, whose page omits the mark). The note is cited by the page of its
mark, B 312; on which page of the second edition the note itself is printed the map does not
verify. The 1781 page A 256, read on the scan, carries neither the mark nor any note at its foot,
and the transcription of A 257 carries none: the map holds the note as the second edition's. The
Korpus prints the note at the foot of AA III 212 without the line numbers it gives the page's
text; its text carries a double space at four places, where, as the map reads the transcription,
the five printed lines of the note break (after »Welt,«, »intellectuell«, »Gegenstand« and
»(unerachtet«), reduced here to one space. »dieses Ausdrucks«, singular, is read as the expression the marked sentence names, »eines mundi sensibilis und intelligibilis«, the pair; since an intellectual world can stand in for a *mundus intelligibilis* only, the alternative, that the expression is *mundus intelligibilis* alone, is as near, and the resolution is the map's, open to dispute.

**Translation.** »Vortrage« "exposition"; »gemeinhin« "commonly"; »pflegt« "is done", the
habitual »zu thun pflegt« rendered by the passive; »Erkenntnisse« "cognitions", as on every Kant
passage in the map; »Anschauungsart« "kind of intuition"; »die Objecte also« "the objects
therefore"; »unerachtet« "notwithstanding"; »Härte des Lauts« "harshness of the sound". The
first sentence's »denn« clause is asserted and gives the ground of the prescription before it.

**What is held and what is not.** The second sentence is a claim set in the row on the division of
the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) since 2026-11-02
([c-kant-objects-of-one-or-the-other-kind-of-intuition-must-be-called-intelligible-or-sensible](../../c/kant-objects-of-one-or-the-other-kind-of-intuition-must-be-called-intelligible-or-sensible/)),
for what it says the division of objects into sensible and intelligible goes by. The first
sentence, the prescription not to use the expression of an intellectual world, with its ground
that only cognitions are intellectual or sensitive, is in the passage and in no claim; the claim
on the second sentence names the ground in its body.
