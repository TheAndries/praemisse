is stated without argument in that place. The parallel ground for time, conclusion (a)'s second sentence, that time as a determination or order attaching to the things themselves could not precede the objects as their condition and be cognized a priori through synthetic propositions (A 32–33 / B 49, in the map since 2026-11-05, [c-kant-time-as-a-determination-attaching-to-things-themselves-could-not-precede-the-objects-and-be-cognized-a-priori](../../c/kant-time-as-a-determination-attaching-to-things-themselves-could-not-precede-the-objects-and-be-cognized-a-priori/)), says of time what this sentence says of determinations in general; no connective joins them, and no link is set.
