The sixth sentence of the section (AA 3:366.27–29), asserted, »Es muß«, and the one sentence of its passage whose main clause is not in the subjunctive. The text glosses its own term, »d. i. ein Gesetz ihrer
Causalität«, and the statement keeps the gloss. »wirkende Ursache« is »efficient cause«. The
sentence is general, of every efficient cause; the two sentences after it apply it to »einem
Subjecte der Sinnenwelt« and give that subject two characters
([c-kant-empirical-character-connects-actions-by-laws-of-nature](../../c/kant-empirical-character-connects-actions-by-laws-of-nature/),
[c-kant-intelligible-character-cause-of-actions-as-appearances](../../c/kant-intelligible-character-cause-of-actions-as-appearances/)),
drawn by »Und da würden wir«, in the subjunctive; the map records the step and has not
formalized it, since the general sentence does not by itself yield two characters.
