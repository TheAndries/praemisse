translation: "- What? and others even say that the external world is the work of our organs? But then our body, as a piece of this external world, would be the work of our organs! But then our organs themselves would be - the work of our organs! This is, as it seems to me, a thorough reductio ad absurdum: supposing that the concept causa sui is something thoroughly absurd. Consequently the external world is not the work of our organs -?"
