The third sentence of Kant's footnote at B xxvi
([the note](../../p/krv-b-xxvi-footnote/)). The statement resolves »einem solchen
Begriffe« as a concept that is a possible thought, the concept of the sentence before
([c-kant-thinking-requires-only-that-i-not-contradict-myself](../../c/kant-thinking-requires-only-that-i-not-contradict-myself/)),
drops the »aber« that contrasts this sentence with that one, renders the parenthesis's
apposition of »reale Möglichkeit« to »objective Gültigkeit« as "that is", and resolves »die erstere«, the former, as the possibility that sentence gave such a concept, which
the parenthesis calls merely the logical; the parenthesis is kept inside the statement, its
»denn« as "for", and its ground is not formalized as an argument, since what it grounds is
the gloss of »objective Gültigkeit« as »reale Möglichkeit« and not the sentence's
requirement. The sentence does not say what the something more is; the next sentence says
where it may lie
([c-kant-the-more-required-may-lie-in-practical-sources-of-cognition](../../c/kant-the-more-required-may-lie-in-practical-sources-of-cognition/)),
and the two are joined by the resumptive »Dieses Mehrere«, not by an inferential
connective, so no `depends_on` is set between them. The sentence names no concept in
particular; that it bears on the concept of a thing in itself is read from the place of the
note's mark after »denken können«, said on the comparison page. No Schopenhauer or
Nietzsche statement in the map speaks of the objective validity or the real possibility of
a concept; the nearest, JGB 21, calls »Ursache« and »Wirkung« fictions for designation
([c-nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions](../../c/nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions/)),
which is not a statement of what a concept's objective validity requires; so no shared
premise and no attack link is set, and a reader may dispute the absence.
