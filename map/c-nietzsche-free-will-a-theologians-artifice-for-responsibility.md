The first sentence of section 7 after its title line, »Irrthum vom freien Willen.«,
whole; the dash before »das anrüchigste« is rendered as a comma in the statement and kept
in the quotation, and the text's closing dots are dropped from the statement, both named
here. The sentence is in the first person plural, »Wir haben heute«, which is kept; »in
ihrem Sinne« is the theologians' sense of »"verantwortlich"«, the word in Nietzsche's
quotation marks, which the statement keeps. What the sentence says the concept is for,
making humanity responsible and so dependent, the next sentences of the section develop
([c-nietzsche-doctrine-of-the-will-invented-for-punishment](../../c/nietzsche-doctrine-of-the-will-invented-for-punishment/), [c-nietzsche-men-thought-free-to-be-judged-and-punished](../../c/nietzsche-men-thought-free-to-be-judged-and-punished/)); the text joins them by no connective and the map sets no
`depends_on`. The comparison [x-the-will](../../x/the-will/) sets the sentence in its
row on whether the will is free, after JGB 21's sentences, since 2026-10-24: it names the
concept the row asks after, and the sense of »frei« it speaks of is being made »"verantwortlich"« and
dependent, which the section's later sentences tie to judging and punishing, and which the
paragraph on freedom there says is neither Kant's »jenem nicht
unterworfen« of B xxvii nor Schopenhauer's »frei von allen Formen« of § 23. No attack
link is set on Kant's B xxvii–xxviii, where the very same will is thought »als einem
Dinge an sich selbst angehörig ... mithin als frei« if the critique is right
([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)), nor on the
antinomy's resolution, where »dieses thätige Wesen« would be »von aller
Naturnothwendigkeit ... unabhängig und frei«
([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)): this
sentence says what the concept »"freier Wille"« is and what it is for, and denies no
statement that a will is free; whether Kant's »frei« is the concept Nietzsche names no
passage in the map says, and the map does not say it. Nor on Schopenhauer's will as thing
in itself »völlig frei von allen Formen«
([c-schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance](../../c/schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance/)), a freedom from the forms of
representation, which the sentence does not speak of. Nor is a link set to JGB 21's
»Unbegriff "freier Wille"« ([c-nietzsche-strike-the-unfree-will-with-the-free-will](../../c/nietzsche-strike-the-unfree-will-with-the-free-will/)),
the same term in the same quotation marks three years earlier: the two sentences say
different things of it, JGB 21 that it is to be struck from the head, this that it is a
theologians' artifice, and neither text names the other. A reader who holds that this
sentence denies a statement in the map may dispute the absence.
