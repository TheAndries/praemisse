The ground clause of the one sentence of the passage (AA 3:210.24–26; B 308–309), »Da nun eine
solche, nämlich die intellectuelle Anschauung, schlechterdings außer unserem Erkenntnißvermögen
liegt«, a causal subordinate clause whose main clause follows by »so«. The text asserts it as the
ground on which it draws that main clause, and the map reads a »da ..., so« clause as asserted, as
it read A 538's »da« clause on 2026-10-19
([c-kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances](../../c/kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances/)).
»Da nun«, "Now since", is dropped from the statement, so that it reads alone, and named here.
»eine solche« points back to the sentence before this one, the last of
[KrV B 308](../../p/krv-b308-at-the-noumenon-all-use-and-meaning-of-the-categories-cease-and-the-possibility-of-a-thing-proved-only-by-a-corresponding-intuition/),
»eine andere Anschauung als die sinnliche«
([c-kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense](../../c/kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense/));
the statement resolves it by name, "an intuition other than the sensible", the resolution the map's,
and keeps the text's own »nämlich die intellectuelle«, so that the identification of that other
intuition with the intellectual is Kant's and not the map's. »schlechterdings« "absolutely", as JGB 19's »schlechterdings« is rendered, where B xxviii's
»schlechterdings unmöglich wären« is "utterly impossible" on that passage, the two renderings named
here; »außer
unserem Erkenntnißvermögen liegt« "lies outside our faculty of cognition", as on the passage. The
clause names neither the thing in itself nor a noumenon; it says where, in relation to our faculty of
cognition, the intellectual intuition lies.

The clause is a premise of the map's argument
[a-kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience](../../a/kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience/),
named in `supports`, which formalizes the step the sentence draws from it by »so«, that the use of
the categories can in no way reach beyond the limit of the objects of experience
([c-kant-use-of-the-categories-can-in-no-way-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience](../../c/kant-use-of-the-categories-can-in-no-way-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience/)),
on two bridges the argument names as the map's. Nothing in the text draws this clause from a claim in
the map, »Da nun« marking it as a ground and not as drawn, and no `depends_on` is set. The nearest
sentences in the map are these, and no link is set from any. B 307's second sentence, carried in the
statement of [c-kant-noumenon-negative-only](../../c/kant-noumenon-negative-only/), says of the same
intuition, »nämlich die intellectuelle«, that it is »nicht die unsrige« and that »wir auch die
Möglichkeit nicht einsehen können« ([KrV B 307–308](../../p/krv-b307/)), where this clause says that
it lies absolutely outside our faculty of cognition; that not being ours and lying outside our faculty
of cognition are one is the map's reading, this clause's »eine solche« points to B 308's third
sentence and not to B 307, no connective joins the two places, and no link is set. B 310's sentence
that we have no intuition, not even the concept of a possible one, through which objects could be
given to us outside the field of sensibility
([c-kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us](../../c/kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us/))
denies us every non-sensible intuition where this clause places the intellectual one outside our
faculty; that the two are one denial is the map's reading, in another paragraph, joined by no
connective. B 310's sentence that one cannot assert of sensibility that it is the only possible kind
of intuition ([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-not-contradictory](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-not-contradictory/))
says what cannot be asserted of sensibility, where this clause says where the intellectual intuition
lies in relation to our faculty; neither names the other's subject, and no link is set. In the same
row, B 310's sentence that the concept of a noumenon is necessary so as not to extend sensible
intuition over things in themselves
([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-necessary-to-restrict-sensible-cognition](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-necessary-to-restrict-sensible-cognition/))
speaks of the sensible intuition's reach and not of another intuition's. In no row, A 42 / B 59's
sentence that we can have cognition of no object as a thing in itself but only as an object of
sensible intuition ([c-kant-objects-known-only-as-appearances](../../c/kant-objects-known-only-as-appearances/))
says through which intuition we cognize, where this clause says which we lack; B xxvii–xxviii's
critical distinction of the two kinds of representation, the sensible and the intellectual, under
»wenn« ([c-kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds](../../c/kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds/)),
is the one other Kant statement in the map naming an intellectual kind, of representation and not of
intuition; and B xxvii–xxviii's sentence that I can lay no intuition under my concept of freedom
([c-kant-freedom-cannot-be-cognized-by-speculative-reason](../../c/kant-freedom-cannot-be-cognized-by-speculative-reason/))
lacks an intuition for one concept where this clause places one kind of intuition outside our
faculty; no connective joins any of them to this clause, and no link is set. Of the other
thinkers, § 22's immediate cognition of the will
([c-schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known/))
is not said in any passage in the map to be an intuition other than the sensible, as the node of
B 308's third sentence says, and no link is set; no Nietzsche statement in the map names an intuition
other than the sensible. Each absence is open to dispute.

Set in no row of the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), since it names
neither the thing in itself nor a noumenon, as B 308's second sentence, on how the possibility of a
thing is proved, is set in no row; named there under *What the three say* since 2026-11-04.
