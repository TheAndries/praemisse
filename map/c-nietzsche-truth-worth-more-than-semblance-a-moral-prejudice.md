The first sentence of the passage. »Schein« is "semblance", as the GD passages render it. The
sentence is about the worth of truth over semblance and the proof of the assumption, not about
whether there is a true world; the fourth proposition of »Die ›Vernunft‹ in der Philosophie« 6
([c-nietzsche-dividing-world-true-and-apparent-is-decadence](../../c/nietzsche-dividing-world-true-and-apparent-is-decadence/)) diagnoses the division of the
world, and this sentence diagnoses a valuation, that truth is worth more than semblance, which
the map sets beside the division and does not identify with it; no passage joins the two. No Kant or Schopenhauer statement in the map says that truth is worth more than
semblance, so no attack link is set.
