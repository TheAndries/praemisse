The fourth sentence of the passage (AA 3:369.21–23), the paragraph's last, asserted, »ist ... nicht zu erwarten«, rendered "is not to be expected". »Eine ursprüngliche Handlung« is "an original action", the adjective kept; its relative clause, »wodurch etwas geschieht, was vorher nicht war«, is kept whole as what the sentence means by it, and the map adds no gloss; »Causalverknüpfung der Erscheinungen« is "causal connection of appearances". The sentence closes the paragraph after the »Also« sentence ([c-kant-all-actions-of-natural-causes-in-the-temporal-sequence-are-themselves-effects-presupposing-their-causes-in-the-temporal-series](../../c/kant-all-actions-of-natural-causes-in-the-temporal-sequence-are-themselves-effects-presupposing-their-causes-in-the-temporal-series/)) and is joined to it by no connective, so no link is set; the paragraph's first sentence ([c-kant-among-the-causes-in-appearance-nothing-can-begin-a-series-absolutely-and-of-itself](../../c/kant-among-the-causes-in-appearance-nothing-can-begin-a-series-absolutely-and-of-itself/)) denies of the causes in appearance a beginning absolutely and of itself, and this one denies of the causal connection of appearances an original action, the two in other words, named here, no link set. The idea reason creates for itself at A 533, of a spontaneity »die von selbst anheben könne zu handeln, ohne daß eine andere Ursache vorangeschickt werden dürfe« ([c-kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself](../../c/kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself/)), is an idea whose content this sentence says is not to be expected from the causal connection of appearances; whether an original action in this sentence's sense is that spontaneity no connective says, the identification would be the map's, and no link is set. Schopenhauer's § 55 asserts that »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)) and that the person »ist nie frei« ([c-schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will](../../c/schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will/)), and § 23 that every appearance of the will is »durchaus dem Satz vom Grunde unterworfen« ([c-schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one](../../c/schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one/)); "necessary", "subject to the principle of sufficient reason" and this sentence's words are not one proposition in the two texts' words, and the convention enters a shared premise only where both statements assert one, so none is entered, one of the nearest unset, beside the paragraph's second sentence and, since 2026-11-08, A 542–543's sentence that the law of nature is a law of the understanding from which it is permitted under no pretext to except any appearance ([c-kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance](../../c/kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance/)), and a reader may dispute the absence on either node. Nietzsche's JGB 21 has »Der "unfreie Wille" ist Mythologie« ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)), which says what the unfree will is and names no cause in appearance and no series, so it denies nothing this sentence states; its »causa sui« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)) is a cause of itself, and whether what begins a series of itself is a causa sui no passage in the map says, the identification would be the map's, as said on the A 533 node, and no attack is set. JGB 21's »Causal-Verknüpfung« is the one word of Nietzsche's in the map that this sentence's »Causalverknüpfung« shares: a thinker who feels compulsion and unfreedom in all causal connection betrays a lack in himself ([c-nietzsche-feeling-unfreedom-in-causal-connection-a-symptom](../../c/nietzsche-feeling-unfreedom-in-causal-connection-a-symptom/)), which says what such feeling betrays and nothing of what is to be expected from the causal connection of appearances; JGB 21's cause and effect as conventional fictions ([c-nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions](../../c/nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions/)), its in-itself with nothing of causal connections ([c-nietzsche-in-the-in-itself-nothing-of-causal-connections](../../c/nietzsche-in-the-in-itself-nothing-of-causal-connections/)) and its invented causes and succession ([c-nietzsche-projecting-sign-world-as-in-itself-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-projecting-sign-world-as-in-itself-is-mythology/)) speak of use, of the in-itself and of us, where this sentence speaks of appearances; none denies it in its words, and no attack is set. The sentence names neither the will nor freedom, and is set in no row of either comparison, as the paragraph's other three sentences are; the comparison on the will names it in its prose under *On freedom*.
