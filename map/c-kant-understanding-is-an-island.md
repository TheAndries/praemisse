A 235–236 / B 294–295 (AA 3:202.15–19). The image is Kant's own and the claim keeps it as
an image: the "unveränderliche Grenzen" are set "durch die Natur selbst", and what lies
beyond is "der eigentliche Sitz des Scheins", not a further land. The literal content, that
the understanding's principles serve only for experience, is the next passage (3:203.04–09)
and its own claim.
