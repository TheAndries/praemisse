JGB 19, the seventh sentence: »Das, was "Freiheit des Willens" genannt wird, ist wesentlich der
Überlegenheits-Affekt in Hinsicht auf Den, der gehorchen muss: "ich bin frei, "er" muss gehorchen" -
dies Bewusstsein steckt in jedem Willen, und ebenso jene Spannung der Aufmerksamkeit ... und was
Alles noch zum Zustande des Befehlenden gehört.« The statement is the sentence, its colon and dash kept, so that »und ebenso« joins »steckt in jedem Willen« and the tension, the gaze, the valuation and the certainty are said to lie in every will, not to be what is called freedom of the will; »genannt wird« is
kept as "is called", so that the sentence says what the name names and does not use the name in its
own voice; »wesentlich« is "essentially"; the list after the colon stands in the order of the text,
with »was Alles noch« as "whatever else"; the quotation marks are Nietzsche's, in the
transcription's one kind, and the inner marks on »er« are kept (said on the passage). The sentence
follows the opening's three ingredients of willing
([c-nietzsche-willing-contains-feelings-thought-and-affect](../../c/nietzsche-willing-contains-feelings-thought-and-affect/)), of which the third is »jener
Affekt des Commando's«, and names an affect defined by its relation to the one who must obey, »in Hinsicht auf Den, der gehorchen
muss«; no connective joins it to the sixth sentence and no `depends_on` is set. The aphorism's close
gives »"Freiheit des Willens"« a second gloss, the word for a state of pleasure of the one who wills
([c-nietzsche-freedom-of-the-will-the-word-for-the-pleasure-of-the-one-who-commands](../../c/nietzsche-freedom-of-the-will-the-word-for-the-pleasure-of-the-one-who-commands/)); the two sentences are joined by no connective, and the map does not say whether the
affect and the state are one, since no sentence does. On the [comparison on the will](../../x/the-will/)
the claim is set in the row on whether the will is free, for the words »Freiheit des Willens« and »ich
bin frei«, the sentence saying what is so called. No attack link is set. Kant's »frei« at B xxvii–xxviii
is a predicate of the very same will thought as belonging to a thing in itself, if the critique is right
([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)), and the antinomy's »frei« is
independence from natural necessity, under »würde«
([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)); this sentence says
what is called freedom of the will is an affect and a consciousness, and no Kant statement in the map
says that it is not, or speaks of a consciousness of freedom at all (B xxviii has freedom thought, »so
kann ich mir doch die Freiheit denken«, not a consciousness of it). Schopenhauer's »frei« in § 23 is
freedom from the forms of appearance ([c-schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance](../../c/schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance/)), which
the sentence does not speak of; his sentences on self-consciousness
([c-schopenhauer-thing-in-itself-reached-only-through-self-consciousness](../../c/schopenhauer-thing-in-itself-reached-only-through-self-consciousness/)) say what
self-consciousness makes known, not what is called freedom of the will. No Kant or Schopenhauer
statement in the map speaks of commanding, obeying or superiority. JGB 21's »"Freiheit des Willens"«
»in jenem metaphysischen Superlativ-Verstande«
([c-nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui](../../c/nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui/)) has the same term in another
aphorism, with a qualification this sentence does not carry; the map does not identify what the two
aphorisms say of the term and sets no link, since neither names the other. A reader who holds that this sentence denies B xxvii–xxviii, that what is called freedom of the will is a predicate of a will as belonging to a thing in itself, may dispute the absence of the link; the denying words are not inside the statement.
