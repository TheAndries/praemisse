The sixth and last sentence of the passage (AA 3:213.18–21; A 257 / B 313), the close of the
paragraph, asserted outright: it states a question and says of it, in a relative clause that is
indicative, »welche Frage wir verneinend beantwortet haben«. The statement keeps the question in
the text's indirect form and the text's »wir«, the person the sentence speaks in, and asserts
what the sentence asserts, that the question has been answered in the negative; that the
negative answer, that no transcendental use of the understanding going to the noumenon as an
object is possible, is thereby given as Kant's own is how the map reads a sentence that reports
its author's answer as given, said here and open to dispute. The sentence's »also«, which draws
the question from the sentence before it, is dropped from the statement, and »jenem empirischen
Gebrauche« is resolved as the use of understanding and reason »in Ansehung der Erscheinungen«
that the sentence before names, the resolution the map's; the parenthesis is kept. »möglich sei«
is "is possible" and »gehe« "goes", the subjunctive of the indirect question rendered by the
indicative, as on every Kant passage in the map; »auf ... gehe« "goes to"; »Weltbau« "the
structure of the world"; »verneinend« "in the negative". The sentence names the noumenon by the
chapter's word and says of it what the row's criterion asks, what cannot be said of it: that
no transcendental use of the understanding reaches it as an object.
