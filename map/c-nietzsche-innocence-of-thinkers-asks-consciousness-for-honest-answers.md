The third sentence of the aphorism, whole but for its opening »In allem Ernste:«, which the
statement drops, and the colon before »zum Beispiel«, which becomes a comma. The relative »welche« is feminine and takes »die Unschuld« as its antecedent, so the
statement says of the innocence that it permits them; »auch heute noch« is kept as "even
today still", »eigentlich« as "actually", »so entschlossen« as "so resolutely", and the
example questions are kept as the sentence gives them, »ob es "real" sei« with Nietzsche's
quotation marks. The sentence precedes, in the next passage, »Der Glaube an "unmittelbare
Gewissheiten" ist eine moralische Naivetät«
([c-nietzsche-belief-in-immediate-certainties-a-moral-naivete](../../c/nietzsche-belief-in-immediate-certainties-a-moral-naivete/)), which the text joins to
it by no connective, so no `depends_on` is set; the comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/)
sets this sentence, since 2026-10-21, in its row on whether anything is cognized immediately,
for the request to consciousness for honest answers, as the map reads it, the sentence
itself speaking of the innocence of thinkers and of what it permits them. No attack link is
set: the sentence names no one, and what it says is said of thinkers' innocence and their
request, not of what consciousness gives; Schopenhauer's »ein durchaus unmittelbar
Erkanntes« ([c-schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known/)) is not denied by
it, and the attacks the map holds on that claim are JGB 16's and JGB 19's.
