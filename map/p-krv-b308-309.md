translation: "Now since such an intuition, namely the intellectual, lies absolutely outside our faculty of cognition, the use of the categories can in no way reach beyond the limit of the objects of experience; and to the beings of sense there do indeed correspond beings of the understanding, and there may even be beings of the understanding to which our sensible faculty of intuition has no relation at all, but our concepts of the understanding, as mere forms of thought for our sensible intuition, do not reach these in the least; so what we call noumenon must be understood as such only in the negative sense."

Second-edition text. The sentence before this span (AA 3:210.21–24) says that applying the
categories to objects not regarded as appearances would require an intuition other than
the sensible, and that the object would then be a noumenon in the positive sense.

Translation revised 2026-10-07: "Grenze" (AA 3:210.27), first rendered "boundary", is now
"limit", the rendering every passage of the map uses for *Grenze* as against *Schranke*
(Prolegomena §57, AA 4:352.21–25). Nothing else changed.
