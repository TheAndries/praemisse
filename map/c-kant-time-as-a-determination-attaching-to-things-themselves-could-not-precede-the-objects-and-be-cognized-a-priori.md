The second sentence of [KrV A 32–33 / B 49](../../p/krv-a32-33-b49-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things/), conclusion (a) on time (AA
3:59.23–26), the ground of the sentence before's second limb, that time is not something that
would attach to things as an objective determination ([c-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things](../../c/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things/)). The text's »Was aber das zweite
betrifft, so könnte sie« points to that limb and says what time, taken so, could not do; the
statement drops the frame, renders »sie« by name as time, and keeps »könnte«, "could", and the
whole predicate, »nicht vor den Gegenständen als ihre Bedingung vorhergehen und a priori durch
synthetische Sätze erkannt und angeschaut werden«, as one: the »und« stands inside the negation,
and the map reads what is denied as the conjunction, preceding the objects as their condition and being
cognized and intuited a priori through synthetic propositions, which the distributive reading, that
time could neither precede nor be so cognized, entails; the form needs only the conjunction. »Bestimmung oder Ordnung« "determination or
order"; »den Dingen selbst anhängende« "attaching to the things themselves". The sentence says
what a determination attaching to things could not do, as A 26 / B 42's ground says of
determinations in general that they cannot be intuited before the existence of the things to which
they belong ([c-kant-determinations-not-intuited-a-priori](../../c/kant-determinations-not-intuited-a-priori/)); the two are parallel grounds and are drawn from no connective to each
other, and no link between Kant's claims is set without one. The claim is a premise of the map's
argument [a-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things](../../a/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things/), where it is the conditional `o -> not p`, the atom `o` the statement's
supposition, time as a determination or order attaching to the things themselves, and `p` what it
could not then do; the modal »könnte« is flattened to "could not" being "does not", the flattening
the map's and named there. It is a conditional on a supposition the sentence before denies, and so
shares and denies nothing: no `shares_premise_with` and no attack is set. The claim sits in no row
of the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), naming neither the thing in itself nor what divides it from appearance, and is named
there under *What the three say*.
