The first sentence of the case of the will at B xxvii (AA 3:17.07–12). It is a
counterfactual, »wollen wir annehmen ... wäre gar nicht gemacht, so müßte ... gelten«, and
the statement keeps it as one: Kant does not say that causality holds of all things, but
what would follow were the distinction not made. The distinction is the one B xxvi
calls proved in the analytical part, that we cognize objects only as appearances
([c-kant-objects-known-only-as-appearances](../../c/kant-objects-known-only-as-appearances/),
3:16.34–37), here named »durch unsere Kritik nothwendig gemachte«. What follows from
the counterfactual in the next sentence is drawn by »also«
([c-kant-without-distinction-free-will-is-a-contradiction](../../c/kant-without-distinction-free-will-is-a-contradiction/)). Since 2026-10-28 the claim is set also on the comparison on the thing in itself, in the row on the division of appearance from thing in itself and what follows from refusing it, beside A 536's »sind Erscheinungen Dinge an sich selbst, so ist Freiheit nicht zu retten« ([c-kant-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved](../../c/kant-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved/)), the case the preface supposes being the case the resolution supposes, as the map reads the two; the cell is set for what follows from refusing the division, the row's second question. The two places draw the consequence by no connective from each other, and no link is set between them.
