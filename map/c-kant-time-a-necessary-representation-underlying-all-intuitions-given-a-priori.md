The first, third, fifth and tenth sentences of [KrV A 31 / B 46–47](../../p/krv-a31-b46-47-time-a-necessary-representation-given-a-priori/), the second
and third points of the metaphysical exposition of time (AA 3:57.27–28, 3:57.30, 3:58.01–03,
3:58.12–14), joined in the statement in the text's order with the sentences between them left out:
the second, the ground of the first, that one cannot annul time itself in regard to appearances
though one can take the appearances out of time (3:57.28–30), the fourth, that in time alone is
all actuality of appearances possible (3:57.30–58.01), and the sixth to ninth, that on this
necessity a priori rests the possibility of apodictic principles of the relations of time, which
cannot be drawn from experience (3:58.04–12), are in the passage and in no claim. The statement
keeps the third sentence's »also«, "therefore", since the sentence it draws from, the first, is in
the statement; the fifth's parenthesis is kept in its parentheses, and its »Diese« and »sie selbst« are resolved by
name as the appearances, the fourth sentence's »Erscheinungen«, in the passage and in no claim, and
as time; the tenth's »Diese Grundsätze« is resolved as "the apodictic principles of the relations of
time", the words of the sixth sentence, in the passage and in no claim, the apposition the map's
resolution of the demonstrative and named here and on the argument's atom `t`; the tenth's »derselben« and
»dieselbe« are resolved by name as experience, the noun of the sentence before, in the passage.
Everything in the statement is indicative and asserted in Kant's own voice. The parallel for space
is the second and third points of § 2, [c-kant-space-a-priori](../../c/kant-space-a-priori/), A 24 / B 38, whose first sentence,
»Der Raum ist eine nothwendige Vorstellung a priori, die allen äußeren Anschauungen zum Grunde
liegt«, this claim's first sentence matches word for word but for »a priori« and »äußeren«: time
underlies all intuitions, space outer ones.
