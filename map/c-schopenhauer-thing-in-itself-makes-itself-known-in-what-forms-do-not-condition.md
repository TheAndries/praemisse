§ 24, second paragraph, the sentence beginning »Umgekehrt aber«. The sentence states where the thing in itself »sich unmittelbar ... kund giebt«, not that it is cognized immediately, which § 22 says of the will ([c-schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known/)); the two are distinct statements and the map links neither to the other. No attack link is set on Kant's A 42 / B 59 ([c-kant-things-in-themselves-unknown](../../c/kant-things-in-themselves-unknown/)): »sich kund geben« says where the thing in itself makes itself known, not that it is cognized, and § 24's own later sentences keep the forces »qualitates occultae«; a reader may dispute the absence. The sentence before it says what in the appearance is conditioned by the forms, plurality, change, matter, and that all of it attaches to the form, not to what appears. What follows for the sciences is [c-schopenhauer-mathematics-and-etiology-never-penetrate-inner-nature](../../c/schopenhauer-mathematics-and-etiology-never-penetrate-inner-nature/).
