§ 25, the third sentence. The text draws it conditionally, »Ist nun dieses Ding an sich ... der Wille; so liegt er«, with the antecedent qualified as what Schopenhauer believes he has »hinlänglich nachgewiesen und einleuchtend gemacht« in § 21 ([c-schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself/)); the statement keeps the conditional and does not discharge it. It is the conclusion of the checked argument [a-schopenhauer-will-knows-no-plurality](../../a/schopenhauer-will-knows-no-plurality/), atom for atom. The sentence goes on, »ist folglich einer; doch ... nicht wie ein Individuum, noch wie ein Begriff Eins ist«, which § 23 already states ([c-schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one](../../c/schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one/), »wie schon gesagt«); that further step, from no plurality to unity, is not in this claim or in the argument.
