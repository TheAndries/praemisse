The second sentence of the passage (AA 3:369.14–19), one by the map's count, its semicolon joining two halves. The first half asserts what every action as appearance is, under the restriction »so fern sie eine Begebenheit hervorbringt«, kept as "insofar as it brings forth an event"; »selbst Begebenheit oder Eräugniß« is "itself an event or occurrence", the text's two nouns rendered by two, »Begebenheit« "event" as at A 533–534, A 536 and A 539–540 in the map; »welche«, feminine, binds to »Begebenheit« or to »Handlung«, as the map reads it, and not to the neuter »Eräugniß«, where the English "which" follows "occurrence"; »darin die Ursache angetroffen werde« stands in the Konjunktiv of a relative clause, rendered "in which the cause is found", the mood of the German not carried by the English, as the passage says. The second half is drawn from the first by »und so«, kept as "and so", inside the one sentence: »alles, was geschieht, nur eine Fortsetzung der Reihe« with »nur« kept as "only", and »kein Anfang, der sich von selbst zutrüge, in derselben möglich«, the Konjunktiv »zutrüge« kept as "would occur", »derselben« resolved as the series. The statement is the sentence whole, so that the step by »und so« stays inside it, in the text's own words, and is not formalized. What this sentence asserts outright A 541 says under »würde«, that the effects in the world of sense are not allowed to begin of themselves, being always determined beforehand through empirical conditions in the preceding time and possible only as a continuation of the series of natural causes ([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)), »nur als eine Fortsetzung der Reihe der Naturursachen« there and »nur eine Fortsetzung der Reihe« here, the same words; named here and on that node, no link set, no connective drawing the one from the other. A 534 says, under »wenn alle Causalität in der Sinnenwelt bloß Natur wäre«, that every event would be determined by another in time according to necessary laws ([c-kant-annulling-transcendental-freedom-would-destroy-all-practical-freedom](../../c/kant-annulling-transcendental-freedom-would-destroy-all-practical-freedom/)), what this sentence asserts of every action as appearance, under a condition there; named, no link set. A 536 says, of the case in which appearances are things in themselves, that nature is the complete cause of every event, a sentence in the passage [p-krv-a536-537-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved](../../p/krv-a536-537-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved/) and in no claim, and this sentence asserts of appearances as such that every action as appearance presupposes a state in which its cause is found. Schopenhauer's § 55 asserts that »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)) and that the person »ist nie frei« ([c-schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will](../../c/schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will/)), and § 23 that every appearance of the will is »durchaus dem Satz vom Grunde unterworfen« ([c-schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one](../../c/schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one/)); "necessary", "subject to the principle of sufficient reason" and this sentence's words are not one proposition in the two texts' words, and the convention enters a shared premise only where both statements assert one, so none is entered, the nearest unset, and a reader may dispute the absence on either node. Nietzsche's JGB 21 has »Der "unfreie Wille" ist Mythologie« ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)), which says what the unfree will is and names no cause in appearance and no series, so it denies nothing this sentence states; its »causa sui« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)) is a cause of itself, and whether what begins a series of itself is a causa sui no passage in the map says, the identification would be the map's, as said on the A 533 node, and no attack is set.
