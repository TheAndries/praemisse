The second sentence of the section's fourth paragraph (AA 3:368.02–10), subjunctive in its
main clauses, »würde ... sagen«, »würde gültig sein«, kept as »would«, with the ground »weil
sie ... vorher bestimmt und ... möglich sind« indicative and asserted. »ihm« and »es« bind to
»dieses thätige Wesen« of the sentence before
([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)),
and the statement names it. The sentence says two things of the beginning and keeps them
apart: the being begins its *effects* in the world of sense »von selbst«, and the *action*
does not begin »in ihm selbst«, since in it nothing happens; and the effects do not thereby
begin of themselves, being determined beforehand by empirical conditions »vermittelst des
empirischen Charakters (der bloß die Erscheinung des intelligibelen ist)«. The parenthesis is
the one clause of the section that says what the empirical character is to the intelligible
one, its appearance, and the statement keeps it. The comparison on the will sets this
sentence in the row on the will as the cause of the body's actions, for the words
»Wirkungen« and »anfange«, as what Kant says of how such a being's effects begin; the word
»Wille« does not occur in the section.
