The seventh sentence of the passage (AA 3:365.30–35). What is asserted is that the effect »kann ... angesehen werden«, can be regarded, as free in one respect and as necessary in another; the sentence does not assert that it is free, and the statement keeps the modal. »also« draws the sentence from the sixth, that an intelligible cause is with its causality outside the series while its effects are in it, and from the fifth and fourth before it, all in the passage and in no claim, so no `depends_on` is set; the apposition »eine Unterscheidung, die ... äußerst subtil und dunkel scheinen muß, die sich aber in der Anwendung aufklären wird« is in the passage and dropped from the statement, named here. »intelligibele Ursache« is "intelligible cause", the adjective A 538 defines ([c-kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance](../../c/kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance/)); »Erfolg« "result"; »doch zugleich« "and yet at the same time". The sentence is the nearest in the map to Schopenhauer's § 55, »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig: dasselbe ist an sich Wille, und dieser ist völlig frei, für alle Ewigkeit« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)), the two sides of one thing there and of one effect here: Schopenhauer asserts that every thing is necessary as appearance and free in itself, Kant that the effect can be regarded as free with respect to its intelligible cause and as necessary with respect to the appearances, and the convention sets `shares_premise_with` only where both statements assert one proposition, which a »kann ... angesehen werden« does not; no premise is set, said on both nodes, and a reader who holds that the two assert the same may dispute the absence. It is also, as the map reads the two, the sentence of the map's A 532–537 that A 541's »Freiheit und Natur ... bei eben denselben Handlungen ... zugleich« ([c-kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions](../../c/kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions/)) says under »würde«; no `depends_on` is set, the later section not drawing on this one by a connective. Nietzsche's GD 8 says that nobody gives man his qualities, »noch er selbst« ([c-nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself](../../c/nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself/)), and that the nonsense was taught »als "intelligible Freiheit" von Kant«; whether that phrase names the regarding of an effect as free with respect to its intelligible cause no passage in the map says, and no attack is set, the sentence here asserting what can be regarded and the identification being the map's. Set on the comparison on the will in the row on freedom, since 2026-10-27, for the word »Freiheit« and for »intelligibelen Ursache«.
