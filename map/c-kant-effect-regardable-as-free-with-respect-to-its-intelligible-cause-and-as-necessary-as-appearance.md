The seventh sentence of the passage (AA 3:365.30–35). What is asserted is that the effect »kann ... angesehen werden«, can be regarded, as free in one respect and as necessary in another; the sentence does not assert that it is free, and the statement keeps the modal. »also« draws the sentence from the sixth, that an intelligible cause is with its causality outside the series while its effects are in it, since 2026-11-11 a claim ([c-kant-intelligible-cause-with-its-causality-outside-the-series-its-effects-in-the-series-of-empirical-conditions](../../c/kant-intelligible-cause-with-its-causality-outside-the-series-its-effects-in-the-series-of-empirical-conditions/)), and this node carries the connective as a `depends_on` link to it since that day, as the map reads it, the fifth and fourth sentences before it being the other candidates it could reach back to, the fifth since the same day a claim ([c-kant-intelligible-cause-not-determined-in-its-causality-by-appearances-though-its-effects-appear](../../c/kant-intelligible-cause-not-determined-in-its-causality-by-appearances-though-its-effects-appear/)) on which the sixth depends by its own »also«, the fourth a claim since 2026-10-28; the map has not formalized the step as an argument, and a reader who wants it checked may ask for it to be made one; the apposition »eine Unterscheidung, die ... äußerst subtil und dunkel scheinen muß, die sich aber in der Anwendung aufklären wird« is in the passage and dropped from the statement, named here. »intelligibelen Ursache« is "intelligible cause", the adjective A 538 defines ([c-kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance](../../c/kant-intelligible-is-what-is-not-itself-appearance/)); »Erfolg« "result"; »doch zugleich« "and yet at the same time". The sentence was, until 2026-11-06, the nearest in the map to Schopenhauer's § 55 (since then A 543's second sentence, asserted outright of every action as appearance, [c-kant-every-action-as-appearance-is-itself-an-event-and-everything-that-happens-only-a-continuation-of-the-series](../../c/kant-every-action-as-appearance-is-itself-an-event-and-everything-that-happens-only-a-continuation-of-the-series/), is nearer, said on the comparison on the will under *On freedom*), »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig: dasselbe ist an sich Wille, und dieser ist völlig frei, für alle Ewigkeit« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)), the two sides of one thing there and of one effect here: Schopenhauer asserts that every thing is necessary as appearance and free in itself, Kant that the effect can be regarded as free with respect to its intelligible cause and as necessary with respect to the appearances, and the convention sets `shares_premise_with` only where both statements assert one proposition, which a »kann ... angesehen werden« does not; no premise is set, said on both nodes, and a reader who holds that the two assert the same may dispute the absence.
