Prolegomena §57, AA 4:353.16–22. The contrast is with mathematics and natural science,
which recognize bounds but no limits (4:352.29–33, its own claim); "Grenzen" here is
"limits" in the sense Kant defines at 4:352.21–23, a space outside that encloses a place.
