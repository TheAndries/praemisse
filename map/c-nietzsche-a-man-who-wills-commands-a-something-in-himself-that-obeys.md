JGB 19, the eighth sentence: »Ein Mensch, der will -, befiehlt einem Etwas in sich, das gehorcht
oder von dem er glaubt, dass es gehorcht.« The statement is the sentence; »ein Etwas« is kept as "a
something", and the dash the transcription prints after »der will« is not carried into the statement.
The sentence stands between the one on the affect of superiority ([c-nietzsche-freedom-of-the-will-the-affect-of-superiority-over-him-who-must-obey](../../c/nietzsche-freedom-of-the-will-the-affect-of-superiority-over-him-who-must-obey/)) and the one on the
»Wunderlichste am Willen« ([c-nietzsche-belief-will-and-action-one-an-erroneous-inference](../../c/nietzsche-belief-will-and-action-one-an-erroneous-inference/)),
which opens »Nun aber beachte man« and has »wir« as »zugleich die Befehlenden und Gehorchenden«; no
connective joins the eighth sentence to either, and no `depends_on` is set. On the
[comparison on the will](../../x/the-will/) the claim is set in the row on what the will is, since
the sentence says what a man who wills does, commanding a something in himself, and names willing by
its verb. No attack link is set: Schopenhauer's § 18 says that the act of will and the action of the
body are one and not cause and effect
([c-schopenhauer-act-of-will-and-action-of-body-identical](../../c/schopenhauer-act-of-will-and-action-of-body-identical/)) and § 22 that the will is immediately
known ([c-schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-inferred-but-immediately-known/)); this sentence says of the one who
wills that he commands and of the something in him that it obeys or is believed to obey, and denies
neither, where the ninth and tenth sentences, set as the attack on § 18's identity, do. The »oder von dem er
glaubt« is kept, since the sentence leaves open whether what is commanded obeys. The one who commands
and the something that obeys are not called subject and object, and the map draws no link to JGB 17's
sentence on the subject inferred by grammatical habit
([c-nietzsche-subject-inferred-by-grammatical-habit](../../c/nietzsche-subject-inferred-by-grammatical-habit/)) or to JGB 54's on the »ich« as a synthesis
([c-nietzsche-ich-a-synthesis-made-by-thinking](../../c/nietzsche-ich-a-synthesis-made-by-thinking/)); the »synthetischen Begriffs "ich"« by which
the duality is disregarded is in the ninth sentence, in the passage after this one.
