§ 24, second paragraph, the sentences from »Wenn daher auch alle Mathematik«. The first is a concession, »wenn auch ... so dringen wir ... doch nimmermehr«, and the statement keeps it as »even if«; what remains for every explanation to presuppose, the forces of nature, »das Grundlose«, is in the passage and not in the statement. The third sentence, »Denn es ist eben das Ding an sich«, gives the ground, and the statement joins the two by a colon where the text has »Denn«; the step is not formalized. The claim concerns explanation by the sciences, not inference: what § 19 says of the law of causality, that it never leads beyond representation ([c-schopenhauer-causality-never-leads-beyond-representation](../../c/schopenhauer-causality-never-leads-beyond-representation/)), is a different sentence, and the map links neither to the other. »Qualitates occultae« and »Aetiologie« are Schopenhauer's; »etiology« follows them.
