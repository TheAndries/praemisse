The "Denn sonst" sentence of B xxvii: "For otherwise the absurd proposition would follow
that there is appearance without anything that appears" (AA 3:17.05–07), where "otherwise"
refers back to the reservation that we must be able to think the same objects as things in
themselves (AA 3:17.02–05). Kant gives no further ground for the conditional in this place.
