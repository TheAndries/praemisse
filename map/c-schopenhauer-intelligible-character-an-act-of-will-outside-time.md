The second sentence of the passage. The statement drops the frame, »Um das Verhältniß beider faßlich zu machen, ist der beste Ausdruck jener schon in der einleitenden Abhandlung gebrauchte, daß«, which says where the expression was first used, and keeps the mode of the expression, »zu betrachten sei«, "is to be regarded"; »daher« "hence" is inside the sentence. The nearest Kant statement in the map is that the subject would by its intelligible character stand under no conditions of time, »denn die Zeit ist nur die Bedingung der Erscheinungen, nicht aber der Dinge an sich selbst« ([c-kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time](../../c/kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time/)): Kant's main clause is under »würde«, and his asserted »denn« clause, on what time is the condition of, is not what this sentence states, which is what the intelligible character is to be regarded as; no shared premise is set, and the comparison on the will names the two as the nearest. »Willensakt« makes the character an act of will, which no Kant passage in the map says of the intelligible character. Nietzsche's GD 8 says nobody gives man his qualities, »noch er selbst«, and that the nonsense was taught as »"intelligible Freiheit"« by Kant ([c-nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself](../../c/nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself/)); whether an act of will outside time that is a man's own character is his giving himself his qualities the sentences do not say, the identification would be the map's, and no attack is set; a reader who holds that Nietzsche's sentence denies this one may dispute the absence. Nietzsche's JGB 36 has the world »auf ihren "intelligiblen Charakter" hin bestimmt und bezeichnet« ([c-nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power](../../c/nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power/)), the term in his quotation marks, under »Gesetzt« and of the world, not of a man; it names no thinker, asserts nothing of what the character is, and neither states nor denies this sentence; no link.
