translation: "We have now not only travelled through the land of pure understanding and carefully inspected every part of it, but have also surveyed it and determined for everything in it its place. But this land is an island, enclosed by nature itself within unalterable limits. It is the land of truth (a charming name), surrounded by a wide and stormy ocean, the true seat of illusion, where many a fog bank and many a quickly melting ice feigns new lands and, by ceaselessly deceiving the seafarer roving about on discoveries with empty hopes, entangle him in adventures from which he can never desist and yet can never bring to an end."

The opening of the chapter *Von dem Grunde der Unterscheidung aller Gegenstände überhaupt
in Phaenomena und Noumena* (AA 3:202.08–11), text identical in A and B. "Grenzen"
(3:202.15–16) is rendered "limits", as in the Prolegomena passages that distinguish
*Grenzen* from *Schranken*. The A/B pages are given from the standard concordance; the AA
line is the authoritative locator.
