The fourth sentence of the passage (AA 3:211.28–34; A 255 / B 310–311), asserted outright. »Am Ende aber ... doch«, the paragraph's connective, is rendered "in the end, however" in the quote, »aber« and »doch« merged into one word, and dropped from the statement, so that it reads alone; »solcher Noumenorum«, "such noumena", is resolved in the statement as the noumena of the two sentences before it, things thought not as objects of the senses but as things in themselves, and the resolution is named here; »(für uns)« kept, the parenthesis turned into commas and the change named; »jene« rendered "sensible intuition", the reading the passage gives, with the alternatives, and marks as the map's; »d. i.« rendered by the colon. »einsehen« is "see", as at B 307, where the possibility of
an intellectual intuition is what »wir auch die Möglichkeit nicht einsehen können«
([c-kant-noumenon-negative-only](../../c/kant-noumenon-negative-only/)); the two sentences deny
the same thing in the same verb, the one of the intuition and the other of the noumena, and the
map draws no link between them, since no connective joins the two places. The sentence's
»könne« governs what the missing intuition would do and is kept as "could".

The sentence says what we lack, intuition and even the concept of a possible intuition beyond
sensibility, and what the understanding has, a problematic extension; it does not say that
there are no noumena, which the next sentence's »nicht willkürlich erdichtet« also does not
say ([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use/),
drawn from this sentence by »also«, the one link set). No premise is shared and no attack is
set. Schopenhauer's § 22 and § 18, that the will is »ein durchaus unmittelbar Erkanntes« and the
body given in two ways, set as attacked by JGB 16's sentence on Schopenhauer's »ich will« ([c-nietzsche-schopenhauer-ich-will-a-superstition](../../c/nietzsche-schopenhauer-ich-will-a-superstition/)), not by the contradictio claim this node names, and in the row on immediacy, say that the
thing in itself is cognized without inference, in self-consciousness; whether that cognition is
an intuition of the kind this sentence says we lack is not said in any passage in the map, and
the comparison says so under *On immediacy*; no attack is set from them, the identification
being the map's to refuse. GD Vernunft 6's second proposition, that another kind of reality »ist absolut unnachweisbar« ([c-nietzsche-grounds-for-apparent-establish-reality](../../c/nietzsche-grounds-for-apparent-establish-reality/)), is the nearest Nietzsche statement to the first half of this one: it denies that a reality can be shown, not that the possibility of noumena can be seen, and no link is set. Nietzsche's Fabel, stage 5, that the "true" world is an idea that
»zu Nichts mehr nütz ist, nicht einmal mehr verpflichtend«, and JGB 16's contradictio in adjecto
deny neither that the possibility of noumena is not to be seen nor that the range beyond
appearances is empty for us; GD Vernunft 6's fourth proposition, that dividing the world is
»nur eine Suggestion der décadence«, speaks of the division, set in the row on the division,
and of no range. Each absence is open to dispute.

Set on the comparison on the thing in itself, in the row on what can be said of the thing in
itself, since 2026-10-30, for what the sentence says can and cannot be said of noumena by us.
