A 42 / B 59 states it directly (AA 3:65.17–19), and A 43 / B 60 adds that no degree of
distinctness in our intuition would bring us nearer to the constitution of objects in
themselves (AA 3:65.28–31). Prolegomena §13, Anmerkung II, repeats it while insisting that
the things so unknown are given to us and are real (AA 4:289.03–12).
