The first sentence of the passage (AA 3:369.13–14), asserted, »kann sicherlich nichts sein«, with »sicherlich« kept as "certainly" and the modal of the relative clause, »anfangen könnte«, kept as "could". »Unter den Ursachen in der Erscheinung« is "among the causes in appearance", the causes that are appearances, as the paragraph's next sentence, every action »als Erscheinung«, reads it; the map reads »in der Erscheinung« so and not as "in the appearance" of some one thing. »schlechthin und von selbst« is "absolutely and of itself", the two adverbs kept as two. The words »von selbst anfangen« are those of Kant's definition of freedom in the cosmological sense at A 533, »das Vermögen, einen Zustand von selbst anzufangen«, without the »zu« ([c-kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-the-faculty-of-beginning-a-state-of-itself](../../c/kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-the-faculty-of-beginning-a-state-of-itself/)), so that what this sentence denies of every cause in appearance is, in the definition's words, the faculty that defines that freedom; the identification of »eine Reihe ... anfangen« here with »einen Zustand ... anzufangen« there is by the words »von selbst anfangen« and is the map's, and no `depends_on` is set, the sentence drawing on the definition by no connective. Nearer in words is A 534, where practical freedom presupposes that there lies in our power of choice a causality to produce something »mithin eine Reihe von Begebenheiten ganz von selbst anzufangen« ([c-kant-practical-freedom-presupposes-a-causality-in-our-power-of-choice](../../c/kant-practical-freedom-presupposes-a-causality-in-our-power-of-choice/)), the series by name, inside what practical freedom presupposes; this sentence denies of every cause in appearance what that one says a power of choice would have to hold, the two are named on each other, and no link is set, no connective joining them. »in der Erscheinung« is rendered "in appearance" here, where the map renders the same words "in the appearance" at A 534, B xxvii and A 539, which speak of one thing's cause, will or character in the appearance; here the phrase ranges over the causes, and the divergence is the map's choice. The sentence says outright, in Kant's own voice, what A 541 says under »würde« of the active being's effects, that they are not allowed to begin of themselves in the world of sense ([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)), and what A 539 names in a »daß« clause under its »würde« main clause as the law of all determination in time, that everything that happens finds its cause in the appearances of the preceding state ([c-kant-intellectual-causality-not-in-series-of-empirical-conditions](../../c/kant-intellectual-causality-not-in-series-of-empirical-conditions/)); the two say the same of the causes in appearance in other words, under »würde« there. A 533 states outright the law that everything that happens must have a cause and hence the causality of the cause, which itself happened, in turn a cause ([c-kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-a-pure-transcendental-idea](../../c/kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-a-pure-transcendental-idea/)), and A 532–533's third sentence, in the passage and in no claim, that the causality of the cause of what happens »auch entstanden und Bedarf nach dem Verstandesgrundsatze selbst wiederum eine Ursache«; under that law reason creates the idea of a spontaneity »die von selbst anheben könne zu handeln« ([c-kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself](../../c/kant-reason-creates-the-idea-of-a-spontaneity-that-could-begin-to-act-of-itself/)), an idea whose content this sentence denies of the causes in appearance. All are named here, and no link is set between them, no connective drawing the one from the other. The paragraph's three other sentences are each a claim ([c-kant-every-action-as-appearance-is-itself-an-event-and-everything-that-happens-only-a-continuation-of-the-series](../../c/kant-every-action-as-appearance-is-itself-an-event-and-everything-that-happens-only-a-continuation-of-the-series/), [c-kant-all-actions-of-natural-causes-in-the-temporal-sequence-are-themselves-effects-presupposing-their-causes-in-the-temporal-series](../../c/kant-all-actions-of-natural-causes-in-the-temporal-sequence-are-themselves-effects-presupposing-their-causes-in-the-temporal-series/), [c-kant-an-original-action-is-not-to-be-expected-from-the-causal-connection-of-appearances](../../c/kant-an-original-action-is-not-to-be-expected-from-the-causal-connection-of-appearances/)); this one opens the paragraph, the second is joined to it by no connective, and no link is set between them. Schopenhauer's § 55 asserts that »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)) and that the person »ist nie frei« ([c-schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will](../../c/schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will/)), and § 23 that every appearance of the will is »durchaus dem Satz vom Grunde unterworfen« ([c-schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one](../../c/schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one/)); "necessary", "subject to the principle of sufficient reason" and this sentence's words are not one proposition in the two texts' words, and the convention enters a shared premise only where both statements assert one, so none is entered, one of the nearest unset, beside the paragraph's second sentence and, since 2026-11-08, A 542–543's sentence that the law of nature is a law of the understanding from which it is permitted under no pretext to except any appearance ([c-kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance](../../c/kant-the-law-of-nature-is-a-law-of-the-understanding-from-which-it-is-permitted-under-no-pretext-to-except-any-appearance/)), and a reader may dispute the absence on either node. Nietzsche's JGB 21 has »Der "unfreie Wille" ist Mythologie« ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)), which says what the unfree will is and names no cause in appearance and no series, so it denies nothing this sentence states; its »causa sui« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)) is a cause of itself, and whether what begins a series of itself is a causa sui no passage in the map says, the identification would be the map's, as said on the A 533 node, and no attack is set. The sentence names neither the will nor freedom, and the cells of the comparison on the will's row on freedom are set for »Freiheit«, »frei« and »Willkür«, so it is set in no row; that page names it under *On freedom*, since 2026-11-06, as the map's reading of what the paragraph denies of every cause in appearance.
