Prolegomena §57, second paragraph. The two disjuncts, "gar keine Dinge an sich selbst
einräumen" and "unsere Erfahrung für die einzig mögliche Erkenntnißart der Dinge ...
ausgeben" (4:350.35–351.01), the second expanded by "mithin" (our intuition as the only
possible intuition, our discursive understanding as the archetype of every understanding,
4:351.01–04), are joined by a second "mithin" to "Principien der Möglichkeit der Erfahrung für allgemeine Bedingungen der Dinge
an sich selbst ... gehalten wissen" (AA 4:350.35–351.05). The map reads the "mithin" as
Kant's statement that the first entails the last; the verdict "Ungereimtheit" in the same
sentence is the conclusion of the argument this claim is a premise of.
