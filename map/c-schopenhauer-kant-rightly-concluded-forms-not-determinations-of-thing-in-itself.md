The first sentence of the second paragraph of § 24. It names Kant, calls his conclusion right and asserts it, under »Was auch immer das Ding an sich sei«, which the statement keeps. Kant's own statement of it for space is A 26 / B 42, »Der Raum stellt gar keine Eigenschaft irgend einiger Dinge an sich ... vor, d. i. keine Bestimmung derselben« ([c-kant-space-not-of-things-in-themselves](../../c/kant-space-not-of-things-in-themselves/)); Schopenhauer's word is the same, »nicht Bestimmungen desselben«, and the link `shares_premise_with` is set between the two on the part about space, named in the comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) as the premise that space is no determination of the thing in itself. The Schopenhauer statement asserts more: time and causality too, and that the forms attach to the thing in itself only once it has become representation. The sentences that follow give the ground, »Denn da das Subjekt sie aus sich selbst ... vollständig erkennt und konstruirt«, not formalized; § 23 states the doctrine ([c-schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance](../../c/schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance/)), where the earlier link is set, and a page later calls it »den Sinn der Kantischen Lehre« ([c-schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one](../../c/schopenhauer-will-groundless-and-one/)). The statement's »Zeit« carries the second link `shares_premise_with` on this node, with Kant's A 539 / B 566–569, »denn die Zeit ist nur die Bedingung der Erscheinungen, nicht aber der Dinge an sich selbst« ([c-kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time](../../c/kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time/)), the premise that time is a condition or form of appearances only, and no determination of the thing in itself, entered on the [comparison on the will](../../x/the-will/) since 2026-10-19 and on the comparison on the thing in itself since 2026-10-29; the identification of Kant's »Bedingung« with Schopenhauer's »Bestimmung« is the map's step, said on both pages.
