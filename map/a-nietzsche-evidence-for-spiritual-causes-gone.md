The argument is the second half of »Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 3, from »Inzwischen haben
wir uns besser besonnen« to »Was folgt daraus? Es giebt gar keine geistigen Ursachen! Die
ganze angebliche Empirie dafür gieng zum Teufel! Das folgt daraus!«

What the form captures and what it leaves out. Premises 1 to 3 are the three denials the
section makes in turn, of the will (»bewegt nichts mehr«), the motive (»ein andrer
Irrthum. Bloss ein Oberflächenphänomen des Bewusstseins, ein Nebenher der That«) and the I
(»hat ganz und gar aufgehört, zu denken, zu fühlen und zu wollen«). The atoms gloss each
as "is a cause" in the sense the section's first half gives it: the will as what moves,
the motive as one of the »antecedentia einer Handlung, ihre Ursachen« which were to be
sought in consciousness, and the I as what thinks, feels and wills; premise 2 rests on
»Nebenher der That«, something alongside the deed and so not among its antecedents, and
not on the comparative »eher ... verdeckt, als dass es sie darstellt«, which denies
nothing outright. A reader who holds that "the will moves nothing" leaves it open whether
the will is a cause in some other sense may dispute the gloss. Premise 4 is the map's
bridge and is epistemic, as the text's own step is: the section's first half says the
belief in causality was taken from »diesen drei "inneren Thatsachen"«
([c-nietzsche-belief-in-causation-from-inner-facts](../../c/nietzsche-belief-in-causation-from-inner-facts/)),
and the conclusion's second sentence calls the three »die ganze angebliche Empirie
dafür«; the map reads the two together as saying that the evidence for spiritual causes,
if there is any, is one of the three. The form therefore reaches »Die ganze angebliche
Empirie dafür gieng zum Teufel« and not »Es giebt gar keine geistigen Ursachen«: the step
from no evidence to none existing is Nietzsche's, in the same breath, and the map records
it as a claim→claim dependency it has not formalized
([c-nietzsche-no-spiritual-causes](../../c/nietzsche-no-spiritual-causes/)). The form does
not cover the »folglich« inside premise 1 (moves nothing, hence explains nothing), nor the
sentences after the conclusion on the world »daraufhin geschaffen«, which are not quoted.
On the reading [r-nietzsche-will-to-power-not-asserted](../../r/nietzsche-will-to-power-not-asserted/)
premise 1 denies the antecedent of JGB 36's conditional
([c-nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only](../../c/nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only/));
the other reading takes GD's denial to concern the will as an inner faculty only.
