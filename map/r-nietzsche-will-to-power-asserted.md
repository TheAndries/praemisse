**The texts it rests on.** JGB 36's »vom Gewissen der Methode aus, geboten« and »man muss
die Hypothese wagen« ([p-jgb-36-not-representation](../../p/jgb-36-not-representation/), [p-jgb-36-will-to-power](../../p/jgb-36-will-to-power/)):
the attempt is not merely permitted but commanded, and the hypothesis is one Nietzsche
says must be ventured. The »nämlich des Willens zur Macht, wie es in ein Satz ist«
(read as »mein Satz«, as the passage node says) names the basic form of the will as
Nietzsche's own proposition.

**What it holds.** On this reading the conditional form is the caution of a hypothesis
Nietzsche holds, and the aphorism's last sentence states his position: the world seen
from inside is will to power and nothing besides (Richardson 1996, ch. 1).

**What depends on it.** On this reading [c-nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power](../../c/nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power/)
is Nietzsche's answer to Schopenhauer's [c-schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself/):
the will kept as the one reality, under a new name, and
[c-nietzsche-mechanistic-world-not-representation-in-schopenhauers-sense](../../c/nietzsche-mechanistic-world-not-representation-in-schopenhauers-sense/) is its
starting point; the comparison page can then set three answers to one question side by
side.

**What tells against it.** The aphorism asserts nothing in the indicative: »Gesetzt ...
ist es nicht erlaubt«, »Gesetzt endlich ... so hätte man«, »sie wäre«. Its first premise
is the antecedent »ob wir an die Causalität des Willens glauben«
([c-nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only](../../c/nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only/)), and GD denies it in Nietzsche's
own voice: »Der Wille bewegt nichts mehr, erklärt folglich auch nichts mehr«
([c-nietzsche-will-moves-nothing](../../c/nietzsche-will-moves-nothing/), [c-nietzsche-will-as-cause-the-great-error](../../c/nietzsche-will-as-cause-the-great-error/)).
That is the ground of the other reading, [r-nietzsche-will-to-power-not-asserted](../../r/nietzsche-will-to-power-not-asserted/).
