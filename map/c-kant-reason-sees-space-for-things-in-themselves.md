Prolegomena §57, AA 4:352.25–27, said "gleichsam", as it were, in the terms of the
definition of a limit just given (4:352.21–23): a limit presupposes a space outside the
enclosed place. The restriction "nur auf Erscheinungen" (4:352.27) is the first
paragraph's restriction of cognition to possible experience (4:350.21–26) in other words,
and the concession that reason can have no determinate concept of things in themselves is
[the unknowability claim](../../c/kant-things-in-themselves-unknown/) of A 42 / B 59; the
"space" is what makes the restriction a limit and not a mere bound.
