translation: "There are still harmless self-observers who believe that there are \"immediate certainties\", for example \"I think\", or, as was Schopenhauer's superstition, \"I will\": as it were as if here cognition got hold of its object pure and naked, as \"thing in itself\", and no falsification took place either on the side of the subject or on the side of the object. But that \"immediate certainty\", just like \"absolute cognition\" and \"thing in itself\", includes a contradictio in adjecto, I shall repeat a hundred times: one really should at last free oneself from the seduction of words!"
