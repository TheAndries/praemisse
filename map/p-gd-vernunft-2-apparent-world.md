Insofar as the senses show becoming, passing away, change, they do not lie... But in this Heraclitus will remain eternally right, that being is an empty fiction. The \"apparent\" world is the only one: the true world is only added on by a lie..."

The close of section 2 of the chapter. »hinzugelogen« is rendered "added on by a lie";
»scheinbar« "apparent", as in the Kant passages' »Erscheinung«/"appearance" family, and
»Schein« is "semblance" elsewhere in the map. The transcription's three dots are
Nietzsche's.
