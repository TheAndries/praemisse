The first sentence of § 24. The sentence attributes the doctrine to Kant by name, »von dem großen Kant gelernt«, and asserts it; the statement keeps both. It licenses two namings of the forms »mit gleichem Recht«, the subject's ways of intuiting and qualities of the object so far as it is representation, and the link `shares_premise_with` is set to Kant's A 24 / B 38 ([c-kant-space-a-priori](../../c/kant-space-a-priori/)), whose statement has space as »nicht als eine von ihnen abhängende Bestimmung«, not a determination dependent on the appearances: the premise the comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) names is that space is not dependent on the objects that appear in it, which both assert, Schopenhauer of time and causality too. No link is set to the claim that space is the subjective condition of sensibility ([c-kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility](../../c/kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility/)), which words the form's being the subject's and not its independence of the objects.
