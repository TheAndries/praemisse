The fourth sentence of the passage (AA 3:363.08–11), Kant's own definition, »verstehe ich unter«, which the statement carries as "as Kant understands the term"; the opening »Dagegen«, which sets it against causality according to nature in the sentence before, is dropped from the statement and named here. »deren«, feminine singular, binds to »Freiheit«, read as the faculty just defined, so that it is the faculty's causality that stands under no other cause; a reader who reads it otherwise may dispute the reading. »also« inside the sentence is kept as "therefore"; »bestimmte«, preterite in form, is "that determined it". »Vermögen« is "faculty", as at A 538 ([c-kant-causality-of-a-being-considered-from-two-sides](../../c/kant-causality-of-a-being-considered-from-two-sides/)), and »von selbst« "of itself", as at A 541 ([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)), the same words in the same sense, the definition here and the subject's beginning under »würde« there; no `depends_on` is set between them, the later section not drawing on this sentence by a connective. The definition is positive, a faculty, with a negative clause, not under another cause: Schopenhauer's § 55 says that »Der Begriff der Freiheit ist also eigentlich ein negativer«, its content »bloß die Verneinung der Nothwendigkeit« ([c-schopenhauer-concept-of-freedom-negative](../../c/schopenhauer-concept-of-freedom-negative/)), and this sentence is the Kant statement in the map that says what freedom in one sense is; the two do not assert one proposition and the one does not deny the other in its words, Schopenhauer's being about the concept's content and Kant's a definition of one sense of the word, so no link is set and a reader may dispute the absence. Schopenhauer's will as thing in itself, »frei von allen Formen« ([c-schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance](../../c/schopenhauer-will-free-of-forms-of-appearance/)) and knowing »keine Nothwendigkeit« ([c-schopenhauer-will-not-subject-to-principle-of-sufficient-reason-knows-no-necessity-is-free](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-subject-to-principle-of-sufficient-reason-knows-no-necessity-is-free/)), is free of the forms and of necessity, where this sentence's faculty is free of a determining prior cause; the map does not identify the two freedoms. Nietzsche's JGB 21 has the desire for »"Freiheit des Willens"« in the metaphysical superlative sense be the desire »eben jene causa sui zu sein« ([c-nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui](../../c/nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui/)) and the causa sui »der beste Selbst-Widerspruch« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)); whether a faculty of beginning a state of itself is a causa sui no passage in the map says, the identification would be the map's, and no attack is set, said on both nodes. Set on the comparison on the will in the row on freedom, since 2026-10-27, for the word »Freiheit«, Kant's definition of the cosmological sense being, by the text's order, the first Kant statement in the map that says outright what freedom is, the practical sense's following at A 533–534; the row's subject is the will, which the sentence does not name, and the note says so.
