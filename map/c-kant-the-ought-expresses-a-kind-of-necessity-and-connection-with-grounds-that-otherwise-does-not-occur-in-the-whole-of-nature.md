The second sentence of the passage (AA 3:371.17–19), whole on A 547 by the 1781 edition's scan, opened today; the 1781 edition reads »vorkomt« and sets »Sollen« in a larger face, the map's text not carrying the emphasis; the collation on the passage. The statement is the sentence whole; nothing is dropped. »Das Sollen« is "The ought", the substantive, named on the passage beside the verb's renderings; »drückt ... aus« "expresses"; »eine Art von Nothwendigkeit und Verknüpfung mit Gründen« "a kind of necessity and connection with grounds", »Verknüpfung« "connection" as at A 544; »die in der ganzen Natur sonst nicht vorkommt« "that otherwise does not occur in the whole of nature", »sonst« "otherwise" kept, »die« feminine singular by its verb »vorkommt«, so the kind, the necessity or the connection and not the grounds, read as the kind, »eine Art«, the head of the phrase, the map's reading, the other two named, and the English "that ... does not occur" after "grounds" carries the singular. What is asserted, in the indicative, is of the ought: that it expresses a kind of necessity and connection with grounds that otherwise does not occur in the whole of nature; the sentence says what the ought expresses and that this does not occur in nature otherwise, not that there is such a necessity, the map's reading of »drückt ... aus«. The sentence opens on its subject and is drawn from no other by a connective; the first sentence, that reason's causality is clear from the imperatives ([c-kant-that-reason-has-causality-or-at-least-that-we-represent-one-in-it-is-clear-from-the-imperatives](../../c/kant-that-reason-has-causality-or-at-least-that-we-represent-one-in-it-is-clear-from-the-imperatives/)), precedes it, and the map reads this one as saying what the imperatives express, not as a premise the first is drawn from or a conclusion drawn from it, so no link is set, said on both; the third sentence, that the understanding can cognize of nature only what is there, or has been, or will be, in the passage and in no claim, follows it by no connective.
