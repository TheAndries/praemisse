The second sentence of the passage (AA 3:369.06–07), a conditional whose antecedent Kant does not assert, »Wenn wir der Täuschung des transscendentalen Realismus nachgeben wollen«, and whose consequent is asserted of that case, »so bleibt weder Natur, noch Freiheit übrig«; the statement is the sentence whole, »wollen« kept as "want to", the »so« of the apodosis dropped as the map drops it at A 536. What »der Täuschung des transscendentalen Realismus« is the sentence does not say, and the term, »transscendentalen Realismus« in the sentence's genitive, dependent on »der Täuschung«, the dative object of »nachgeben«, stands in no other passage in the map; the map renders »Täuschung« "deception", as in JGB 36 and GD »Die ›Vernunft‹ in der Philosophie« 6, and does not say what the deception consists in. Nearest in the map, in form and in words, with A 534's conditional weighed below, is A 536's »Denn sind Erscheinungen Dinge an sich selbst, so ist Freiheit nicht zu retten« ([c-kant-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved](../../c/kant-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved/)), a conditional on an unasserted case with freedom lost in its consequent, in the fifth paragraph of section III, the three paragraphs of the section on causality through freedom standing between it and the subsection: whether the case of yielding to the deception of transcendental realism is the case in which appearances are things in themselves no passage in the map says, the identification would be the map's, and the two consequents differ, A 536 leaving freedom not to be saved and, in its next sentence, in [its passage](../../p/krv-a536-537-if-appearances-are-things-in-themselves-freedom-not-to-be-saved/) and in no claim, nature »die vollständige und an sich hinreichend bestimmende Ursache jeder Begebenheit« in that case, where this sentence leaves neither nature nor freedom; no connective joins the two, and no link is set. The sentence before A 536's, on »die zwar gemeine, aber betrügliche Voraussetzung der absoluten Realität der Erscheinungen«, in the same passage and in no claim, has the one word for a deception the map's Kant passages say of a presupposition, »betrüglich«, "deceptive", where this sentence has »Täuschung« of a realism; A 235 / B 294 has the ocean round the island as »dem eigentlichen Sitze des Scheins«, whose ice »den auf Entdeckungen herumschwärmenden Seefahrer unaufhörlich mit leeren Hoffnungen täuscht« ([c-kant-understanding-is-an-island](../../c/kant-understanding-is-an-island/)), the stem of »Täuschung« said inside a figure and naming no realism; whether the presupposition and the realism are one the texts do not say. A 534's »wenn alle Causalität in der Sinnenwelt bloß Natur wäre, ... so würde die Aufhebung der transscendentalen Freiheit zugleich alle praktische Freiheit vertilgen« ([c-kant-annulling-transcendental-freedom-would-destroy-all-practical-freedom](../../c/kant-annulling-transcendental-freedom-would-destroy-all-practical-freedom/)) is likewise a conditional on a case Kant does not assert with a freedom lost in its consequent: its case is all causality in the world of sense being nature, not a realism, and its consequent practical freedom destroyed, not nature and freedom both gone; no connective joins the two, and no link is set. B xxvii's two sentences on the case in which the critique's distinction is not made, that causality would hold of all things in general and that a free will would be an obvious contradiction ([c-kant-without-distinction-causality-holds-of-all-things](../../c/kant-without-distinction-causality-holds-of-all-things/), [c-kant-without-distinction-free-will-is-a-contradiction](../../c/kant-without-distinction-free-will-is-a-contradiction/)), say of another unasserted case what follows for causality and for a free will, not that nature does not remain; and A 541's »So würde denn Freiheit und Natur ... bei eben denselben Handlungen ... zugleich und ohne allen Widerstreit angetroffen werden« ([c-kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions](../../c/kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions/)), under »würde«, has both where this sentence, under its own »wenn«, has neither; none is joined to this sentence by a connective, and no link is set. Schopenhauer's nearest statement is § 55's »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung, als Objekt, durchweg nothwendig: dasselbe ist an sich Wille, und dieser ist völlig frei« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)), which asserts necessity of appearance and freedom of the thing in itself, where this sentence says what a deception leaves; no Schopenhauer passage in the map names a realism, and no premise and no attack is set. Nietzsche's GD »Die ›Vernunft‹ in der Philosophie« 6, in its second proposition, in [its passage](../../p/gd-vernunft-6-four-theses/) and in no claim, has the »"wahre Welt"« built »aus dem Widerspruch zur wirklichen Welt« as »eine scheinbare Welt in der That, insofern sie bloss eine moralisch-optische Täuschung ist«, the one other place in the map's passages where the word »Täuschung« is asserted of something, JGB 36's »Täuschung« standing under »Ich meine nicht als«: Nietzsche's deception is a "true world", Kant's a realism he does not define here, and whether the two name one thing no passage says; since that sentence is in no claim, no link can be set. JGB 21's »Der "unfreie Wille" ist Mythologie« ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)) names the will, which this sentence does not. Nearest in form among Nietzsche's sentences is the Fabel's stage 6, »mit der wahren Welt haben wir auch die scheinbare abgeschafft« ([c-nietzsche-abolishing-true-world-abolishes-apparent](../../c/nietzsche-abolishing-true-world-abolishes-apparent/)), two things gone together, there a true and an apparent world, here nature and freedom, the stage read as Nietzsche's own voice by the owner's decision of 2026-10-10; the two do not deny each other in their words, and no link is set. The sentence names »Freiheit« and is set, since 2026-11-09, in the Kant cell of the row on freedom on the comparison on the will, for that word, as every sentence of the resolution in the map that names it is, after the eleven A 532–537 claims, where the fourth paragraph's sentences, which name neither freedom nor the will, are set in no row; it is not set in the row on the division on the comparison on the thing in itself, where A 536's conditional is set for what follows from taking appearances for things in themselves, since the sentence says what yielding to transcendental realism leaves and not what transcendental realism is, and whether it is taking appearances for things in themselves no passage in the map says; the sentence is named there as the nearest unset, and a reader who holds that the two cases are one may dispute the absence on either node.
