The third sentence of the paragraph, the thesis the rest of it argues: the next sentence opens
with »Denn« and the chain that follows, from what a priori means to the intellect's own forms,
is formalized as [a-schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-are-the-intellects-own-forms](../../a/schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-are-the-intellects-own-forms/), whose conclusion is the paragraph's »Demnach« sentence
([c-schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-and-forms-of-the-intellect-two-expressions-for-one-thing](../../c/schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-and-forms-of-the-intellect-two-expressions-for-one-thing/)). The statement keeps both halves, the uniqueness of the explanation, »gar keine
andere Erklärung ... als«, and the correction, »weniger eine Erklärung, als eben nur der
deutliche Ausdruck der Thatsache selbst«; neither is a premise of the argument, which proves
the synonymy and not the uniqueness. »Diese« are the a priori cognitions, the part named in
the fact. The Schopenhauer sentence that says the same of space in particular is the appendix's
»eine a priori gegebene, folglich subjektive Form unsers Intellekts«
([c-schopenhauer-causality-sensation-and-space-are-subjective](../../c/schopenhauer-causality-sensation-and-space-are-subjective/)), on which the sharing
with Kant is set; this general statement names no form, and the comparison
[x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) says why no link is set on it.
