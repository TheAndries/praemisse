B xxvi draws this as what follows from the result that we cognize objects only as
appearances (AA 3:16.37–17.02). B 308–309 states a parallel restriction on the use of the
categories, which can in no way reach beyond the limit of the objects of experience
(AA 3:210.24–27). Since 2026-11-04 that clause is a claim of its own ([c-kant-use-of-the-categories-can-in-no-way-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience](../../c/kant-use-of-the-categories-can-in-no-way-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience/)), the conclusion of the map's argument [a-kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience](../../a/kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience/); that it and this sentence state one restriction, of speculative cognition of reason there and of the use of the categories here, is the map's reading, no connective joins the preface to the chapter, and no link is set between them.
