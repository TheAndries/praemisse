The »da« clause of the fifth sentence of the section (AA 3:366.22–24), with its own »weil«
clause inside it. »diesen« binds to »Erscheinungen« in the sentence before. The clause
asserts two things in order: that appearances »an sich keine Dinge sind«, and that for that
reason a transcendental object »zum Grunde liegen muß«, one »der sie als bloße Vorstellungen
bestimmt«; the statement keeps the order and the »because«. It is a premise of the map's
checked argument
[a-kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience](../../a/kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience/),
where the two assertions are two atoms. The word is »transscendentaler Gegenstand«, not
»Ding an sich selbst«; B xxvi has a near thought, as the map reads it, that there would otherwise be »Erscheinung ohne etwas wäre, was da erscheint«
([c-kant-appearance-presupposes-something-appearing](../../c/kant-appearance-presupposes-something-appearing/)),
and whether the transcendental object of this sentence is the thing in itself of that one is
not stated in any passage in the map; the map does not say it, and sets no link that would
need it. The sentence says what underlies appearances must do, »bestimmt«, and not what it is.
