GD, »Wie die ›wahre Welt‹ endlich zur Fabel wurde«, stage 6: »Die wahre Welt haben wir
abgeschafft: welche Welt blieb übrig? die scheinbare vielleicht?... Aber nein! mit der
wahren Welt haben wir auch die scheinbare abgeschafft!« The statement is the stage's last
sentence; the self-questioning before it (»welche Welt blieb übrig? die scheinbare
vielleicht?... Aber nein!«) is in the passage. The stage opens by recording the abolition
stage 5 called for (»schaffen wir sie ab!« / »haben wir abgeschafft«); that is a narrative
sequence, not a step that draws one claim from another, so the map sets no dependency on
[c-nietzsche-true-world-idea-refuted](../../c/nietzsche-true-world-idea-refuted/). The stage gives no ground for »mit der wahren
Welt ... auch die scheinbare«; the map reads it with the first proposition of »Die
›Vernunft‹ in der Philosophie« 6, [c-nietzsche-grounds-for-apparent-establish-reality](../../c/nietzsche-grounds-for-apparent-establish-reality/),
where the world called apparent is real, so that what is abolished is the predicate
"apparent", not the world. Kant's [c-kant-without-things-in-themselves-appearance-without-appearing](../../c/kant-without-things-in-themselves-appearance-without-appearing/)
says that without things in themselves there would be appearance without anything that
appears, and treats that as absurd; this stage affirms the antecedent and denies the
consequent as well, rejecting the pair Kant's conditional presupposes, which the
comparison page will set side by side. The map sets
no link between them, since this stage does not state Kant's conditional.

Whose voice. The map reads this stage, with stage 5, as Nietzsche's own, following the
owner's reading of 2026-10-10 (`CHANGELOG.md`): its parenthesis ends in »INCIPIT
ZARATHUSTRA«, and stage 5's names the free spirits, »Teufelslärm aller freien Geister«;
the chapter has no sentence that separates the two. Stage 4 alone keeps its framing as
the positivist stage, for the reason given on
[a-nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate](../../a/nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate/).
It may be disputed like any other reading.
