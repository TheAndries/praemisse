The main clause of the sentence at B xxviii (AA 3:18.06–11). What »denken« comes to is
said in the text itself, »d. i. die Vorstellung davon enthält wenigstens keinen
Widerspruch in sich«, and the statement keeps the »wenigstens«. The claim is conditional
on the critical distinction and the restriction that follows from it, and the statement
keeps the »wenn«; The condition names the distinction of
»Vorstellungsarten«, kinds of representation, where the sentence before named two
meanings of the object; the argument that reaches this claim takes the two as one condition, says that the
identification is the map's, and names the restriction as the second part of the
antecedent,
[a-kant-freedom-thinkable-under-the-critical-distinction](../../a/kant-freedom-thinkable-under-the-critical-distinction/).
B xxviii–xxix then draws the consequence for morality
([c-kant-same-action-in-another-relation](../../c/kant-same-action-in-another-relation/)),
after the supposition of the contrary
([c-kant-if-freedom-unthinkable-morality-yields-to-mechanism](../../c/kant-if-freedom-unthinkable-morality-yields-to-mechanism/)).
