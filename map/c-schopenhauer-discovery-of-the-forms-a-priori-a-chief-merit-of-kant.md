The seventh sentence of the passage. Its »Dieses« points to the sentence before it ([c-schopenhauer-forms-of-all-object-found-from-the-subject-a-priori](../../c/schopenhauer-forms-of-all-object-found-from-the-subject-a-priori/)), and the statement restates what is discovered in that sentence's own words, the forms of every object »a priori in unserm Bewußtseyn liegen«, so that it reads alone; the restatement is the map's resolution of the pronoun and claims nothing the preceding sentence does not. »Hauptverdienst« is "chief merit". The Anhang's opening sentence, »Kants größtes Verdienst ist die Unterscheidung der Erscheinung vom Dinge an sich« (ZA II 513, [c-schopenhauer-kants-greatest-merit-distinction-of-appearance-from-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-kants-greatest-merit-distinction-of-appearance-from-thing-in-itself/)), credits Kant with a distinction, this sentence with a discovery about the forms, and the map does not identify the two merits. Set in the row of [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) on what in the world of appearance is the subject's own, with the preceding sentence, as what Schopenhauer, with Kant's name, credits him with having discovered about the forms. No link is set to any Kant claim: the sentence attributes a discovery and asserts, in its own words, no proposition about space beyond the one the preceding sentence asserts, on which the link is set.
