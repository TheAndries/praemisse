Bedingung der Erscheinungen, nicht aber der Dinge an sich selbst«, is indicative and asserted
in Kant's own voice; no passage of the Aesthetic's section on time (§§ 4–7, A 30–41 / B 46–58) is in the map. »Dieses handelnde Subject« is the subject of the world of sense of the paragraph before, which would have to be granted an intelligible character
