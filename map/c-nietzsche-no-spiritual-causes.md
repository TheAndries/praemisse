GD, »Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 3: »Was folgt daraus? Es giebt gar keine geistigen
Ursachen! Die ganze angebliche Empirie dafür gieng zum Teufel! Das folgt daraus! -« The
claim depends on [c-nietzsche-evidence-for-spiritual-causes-gone](../../c/nietzsche-evidence-for-spiritual-causes-gone/)
by the text's own »Das folgt daraus«: the three denials the section makes are what
»daraus« names, the sentence on the evidence says what they take away, and the map's
argument [a-nietzsche-evidence-for-spiritual-causes-gone](../../a/nietzsche-evidence-for-spiritual-causes-gone/)
reaches the evidence and not the existence; the step from no evidence to no spiritual
causes is Nietzsche's and the map has not formalized it. A reader who wants it checked
may ask for it to be made an argument.
