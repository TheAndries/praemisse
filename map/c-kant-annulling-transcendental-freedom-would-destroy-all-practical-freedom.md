The fifth sentence of the passage (AA 3:364.04–09), a conditional whose antecedent Kant does not assert, »wenn alle Causalität in der Sinnenwelt bloß Natur wäre«, with every main verb under »würde« and »müßten«, kept as "were", "would" and "would have to"; the opening »Man sieht leicht, daß« is dropped from the statement and named here. »mithin« inside the sentence is "hence"; »Erfolg« "result"; »Aufhebung« "annulment", as »aufheben« is "annul" at B xxx. The sentence is drawn by »Denn« from the one after it, that practical freedom presupposes a causality in our power of choice to begin a series of events of itself ([c-kant-practical-freedom-presupposes-a-causality-in-our-power-of-choice](../../c/kant-practical-freedom-presupposes-a-causality-in-our-power-of-choice/)), and `depends_on` names that claim, the connective quoted and the step not formalized. Beside B xxviii, where, supposing morality presupposes freedom and speculative reason had proved it unthinkable, morality would yield to the mechanism of nature ([c-kant-if-freedom-unthinkable-morality-yields-to-mechanism](../../c/kant-if-freedom-unthinkable-morality-yields-to-mechanism/)): both say under a supposition what would go with freedom, morality there and practical freedom here, and the map sets no link, the two statements not asserting one proposition. No Schopenhauer or Nietzsche statement in the map says what the annulment of one freedom would do to another; § 55's »Jedes Ding ist als Erscheinung ... nothwendig« ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)) asserts of every thing what this antecedent supposes of all causality in the world of sense, and then asserts the will free, and the map does not identify Schopenhauer's necessity of every appearance with Kant's »bloß Natur«, so no link is set. Set on the comparison on the will in the row on freedom, since 2026-10-27, for the word »Freiheit«, under its supposition.
