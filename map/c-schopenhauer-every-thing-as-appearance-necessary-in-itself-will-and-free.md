The eighth sentence of § 55, the sentence the passage's seventh calls the point of unity of freedom with necessity. The statement supplies "thing" to »dasselbe« and "will" to »dieser«, their antecedents in the sentence, and keeps the colon. No link to Kant is set. The nearest Kant statements in the map are B xxvii–xxviii, where the very same will is thought as not free in the appearance and as free »als einem Dinge an sich selbst angehörig« ([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)), and the resolution of the third antinomy, where the active being would by its intelligible character be »von aller Naturnothwendigkeit ... unabhängig und frei« ([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)): the first stands under »Wenn aber die Kritik nicht geirrt hat« and has the will »gedacht«, the second under »würde«, as is the antinomy's »Freiheit und Natur ... bei eben denselben Handlungen« ([c-kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions](../../c/kant-freedom-and-nature-together-in-the-same-actions/)), and the convention sets `shares_premise_with` only where both statements assert one proposition; Kant's will there belongs to a thing in itself and is not said to be one. A reader who holds that the two assert the same may dispute the absence. Schopenhauer's »dasselbe ist an sich Wille« asserts of every thing what Kant's B xxvii says of one will under the critique's condition; the map does not identify the two. Nietzsche's GD »Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 8 says »Man ist nothwendig, man ist ein Stück Verhängniss«, in the passage and in no claim, and its claim in the map, that nobody gives man his qualities ([c-nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself](../../c/nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself/)), denies no part of this sentence in its words.
