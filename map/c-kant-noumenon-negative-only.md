B 307 draws the distinction between the two senses (AA 3:209.32–210.02); B 308–309
draws from its ground clause, that the intellectual intuition lies absolutely outside our faculty of
cognition, its clause that the use of the categories can in no way reach beyond the limit of the
objects of experience, and from that by »also« that what we call noumenon must be understood only in
the negative sense (AA 3:210.24–34; the two steps said so since 2026-11-04, when the first became the
map's argument and the second a link, below; until then this paragraph gave the sentence as one step).

Since 2026-10-30 the map holds the two paragraphs that follow at A 254–256 / B 310–312, where the concept is called »bloß ein Grenzbegriff ... und also nur von negativem Gebrauche« ([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use/)) and the division into phenomena and noumena is refused »in positiver Bedeutung« ([c-kant-division-into-phenomena-and-noumena-not-admissible-in-a-positive-sense](../../c/kant-division-into-phenomena-and-noumena-not-admissible-in-a-positive-sense/)), the words of this claim's positive sense at 3:210.02; the identification of the two places' »positiv« is the map's, and no link is set, no connective joining the places. 3:210.35–211.14, between B 308–309 and those paragraphs, is in no passage.
