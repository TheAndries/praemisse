The sixth sentence of the paragraph, a conditional in the text, »Besteht nun dies ...; nun, so
ist damit gesagt«, and a conditional in the statement; the map does not assert its antecedent
in this claim. It is premise 4 of the checked argument [a-schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-are-the-intellects-own-forms](../../a/schopenhauer-cognitions-a-priori-are-the-intellects-own-forms/), and the argument discharges
the antecedent by an assumption named as the map's: that the a priori cognitions consist in
the universal manner in which all the intellect's objects must present themselves to it is what
the Aesthetic's theorems say of space and time, which Schopenhauer counts irrefutable
([c-schopenhauer-theorems-of-transcendental-aesthetic-irrefutable-truths](../../c/schopenhauer-theorems-of-transcendental-aesthetic-irrefutable-truths/)), and no sentence of this paragraph asserts it in so many words. »Dies so in ihm selbst
Vorhandene« is what the two sentences before have reached, the a priori cognitions as the
intellect's own; the statement keeps »thus«.
