translation: "Why might the world that is of any concern to us - not be a fiction? And to one who asks: \"but to a fiction there belongs an author?\" - might it not be answered roundly: Why? Does this \"belongs\" not perhaps also belong to the fiction? Is it not permitted, then, to be by now a little ironical towards the subject, as towards predicate and object? Might the philosopher not rise above faith in grammar? All respect to the governesses: but would it not be time for philosophy to renounce the governesses' faith? -"

The last six sentences of aphorism 34, all questions, from the one after the passage on
truth and semblance ([p-jgb-34-truth-and-semblance](../../p/jgb-34-truth-and-semblance/)) to the end; the dash after
»absagte?« closes the aphorism in the transcription and is kept. »Die uns etwas angeht« is
rendered "that is of any concern to us", keeping »etwas«; the »da« of »wer da fragt« is a
particle and is left unrendered; »mit zur Fiktion« is "also belong to the fiction". The words
»zur Fiktion gehört ein Urheber« are rendered "to a fiction there belongs an author" so that
the next question's »dieses "Gehört"« can be rendered with the same word, "belongs", in
quotation marks, and the play on the word kept; »rund«
"roundly"; »nachgerade« "by now"; »Gläubigkeit an die Grammatik« "faith in grammar";
»Gouvernanten-Glauben« "the governesses' faith". The first three questions are a claim
([c-nietzsche-why-might-the-world-not-be-a-fiction](../../c/nietzsche-why-might-the-world-not-be-a-fiction/)); the last three, on being ironical towards subject, predicate and object, on
rising above faith in grammar and on the governesses, are in no claim. The map holds JGB 17
and 54 on the grammatical subject ([c-nietzsche-subject-inferred-by-grammatical-habit](../../c/nietzsche-subject-inferred-by-grammatical-habit/))
and sets no link from them to these questions, which name grammar and no connective joins
to those aphorisms.
