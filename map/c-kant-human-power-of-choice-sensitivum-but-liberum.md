The fourth sentence of the passage (AA 3:363.33–364.03), asserted in the indicative, with its ground, »weil«, inside the sentence and not formalized; »zwar ... aber« is "indeed ... but", »sondern« twice "rather" and "but rather", »beiwohnt« "dwells in", »von selbst« "of himself" here, since the reflexive is the human being's, where the three passages otherwise have "of itself". The terms are the third sentence's, which is in the passage and in no claim: a power of choice is animal, »arbitrium brutum«, if it can be pathologically necessitated. This is the one Kant statement in the map that asserts of a human faculty outright that it is free: B xxvii–xxviii has the will thought as free under »Wenn aber die Kritik nicht geirrt hat« ([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)), and A 541's active being would be »unabhängig und frei« by its intelligible character under »würde« ([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)); the sentence says of the human »Willkür«, rendered "power of choice", not of the will, and what it is free of is necessitation by sensible impulses, not the law of nature. Schopenhauer's § 55 says that the person, »das vernünftige Thier mit individuellem Charakter«, »ist nie frei, obwohl sie die Erscheinung eines freien Willens ist« ([c-schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will](../../c/schopenhauer-person-never-free-though-appearance-of-free-will/)): that sentence denies the person's freedom, this one asserts the human power of choice's freedom in the practical sense, and whether Schopenhauer's person is the bearer of Kant's »Willkür« and whether »nie frei« denies »liberum« in the sense defined here no passage in the map says; the identification of the two subjects and of the two senses would be the map's, no attack is set, and a reader who holds that § 55's sentence denies this one may dispute the absence, the nearest denial in the map. Nietzsche's GD 7 calls »"freier Wille"« a theologians' artifice for making humanity responsible ([c-nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility](../../c/nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility/)), which says what the concept is for and names no faculty; GD 8 says nobody gives man his »Eigenschaften«, »noch er selbst« ([c-nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself](../../c/nietzsche-nobody-gives-man-his-qualities-not-even-himself/)), which speaks of the giving of qualities, not of determining oneself to action; JGB 21's »Der "unfreie Wille" ist Mythologie: im wirklichen Leben handelt es sich nur um starken und schwachen Willen« ([c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)) denies an unfree will and says what willing is a matter of, not that no faculty of self-determination dwells in man; none denies this sentence in its words, and no attack is set, said on the nodes. Set on the comparison on the will in the row on freedom, since 2026-10-27, for the word »Freiheit« and for »Willkür« and »liberum«.
