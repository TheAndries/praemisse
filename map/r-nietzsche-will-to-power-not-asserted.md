**The texts it rests on.** JGB 36 is conditional throughout: »Gesetzt, dass ...«, »thun
wir das ... so müssen wir den Versuch machen«, »Gesetzt endlich, dass es gelänge ... so
hätte man damit sich das Recht verschafft«, »sie wäre eben "Wille zur Macht"«
([p-jgb-36-will-to-power](../../p/jgb-36-will-to-power/)). Its central premise is the belief in the causality of
the will; JGB 19 already calls the will »etwas Complicirtes« and the philosophers' way of
speaking of it a »Volks-Vorurtheil« ([p-jgb-19-willing-complicated](../../p/jgb-19-willing-complicated/)); GD, »Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 3, says »Der Wille bewegt nichts mehr, erklärt
folglich auch nichts mehr« ([p-gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause](../../p/gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause/)), and »Die
›Vernunft‹ in der Philosophie« 5 calls the belief that »der Wille Etwas ist, das wirkt«
»das grosse Verhängniss von Irrthum« ([p-gd-vernunft-5-will-as-cause](../../p/gd-vernunft-5-will-as-cause/)).

**What it holds.** On this reading Nietzsche sets out what would follow from premises he
does not accept, and the aphorism's conclusion is not his position; the will to power
remains, in the published works, a psychological thesis and not a doctrine about the
world (Clark 1990, ch. 7).

**What depends on it.** On this reading [c-nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power](../../c/nietzsche-world-would-be-will-to-power/)
is not Nietzsche's answer to Schopenhauer's [c-schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-will-alone-is-thing-in-itself/);
his answer is the denial of any thing in itself and of the will as a cause
([c-nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto](../../c/nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto/),
[c-nietzsche-will-moves-nothing](../../c/nietzsche-will-moves-nothing/)), and the comparison page sets two answers and a
refusal side by side rather than three answers.

**What tells against it.** The aphorism says the attempt is »vom Gewissen der Methode
aus, geboten« and that »man muss die Hypothese wagen«; it names the will to power as
Nietzsche's own »Satz« (read as »mein Satz«, as the passage node says); and the readings differ on whether GD's denial of the will as
cause denies the »Willens-Causalität« of JGB 36 or only the will as the inner fact of a
faculty. That is the ground of the other reading, [r-nietzsche-will-to-power-asserted](../../r/nietzsche-will-to-power-asserted/).
The map does not pick a reading (`CHARTER.md` P9).
