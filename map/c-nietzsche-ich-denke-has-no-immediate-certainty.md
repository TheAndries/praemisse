JGB 16: »wegen dieser Rückbeziehung auf anderweitiges `Wissen` hat er für mich jedenfalls
keine unmittelbare `Gewissheit`«. The »er« is »meinen augenblicklichen Zustand« of the
clause before (»um so festzusetzen, was er ist«), not »jenes `ich denke`«, which is
neuter; the statement names the state and says which state it is. The »wegen« draws the
claim from the comparison the sentence before states, and the map formalizes the step as
[a-nietzsche-ich-denke-has-no-immediate-certainty](../../a/nietzsche-ich-denke-has-no-immediate-certainty/);
the ground, the back-reference, is a premise of the argument and not part of this claim.
»jedenfalls« is kept in the statement: the sentence says "at any rate for me", in the
philosopher's mouth, not for the people, whose belief the sentence after returns to (the span ends before it).
