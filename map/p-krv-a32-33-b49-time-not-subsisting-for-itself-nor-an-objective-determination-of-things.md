original: "a) Die Zeit ist nicht etwas, was für sich selbst bestände, oder den Dingen als objective Bestimmung anhinge, mithin übrig bliebe, wenn man von allen subjectiven Bedingungen der Anschauung derselben abstrahirt: denn im ersten Fall würde sie etwas sein, was ohne wirklichen Gegenstand dennoch wirklich wäre. Was aber das zweite betrifft, so könnte sie als eine den Dingen selbst anhängende Bestimmung oder Ordnung nicht vor den Gegenständen als ihre Bedingung vorhergehen und a priori durch synthetische Sätze erkannt und angeschaut werden. Dieses letztere findet dagegen sehr wohl statt, wenn die Zeit nichts als die subjective Bedingung ist, unter der alle Anschauungen in uns stattfinden können. Denn da kann diese Form der innern Anschauung vor den Gegenständen, mithin a priori vorgestellt werden."
translation: "a) Time is not something that would subsist for itself, or would attach to things as an objective determination, and so would remain if one abstracts from all subjective conditions of the intuition of them: for in the first case it would be something that, without an actual object, would nevertheless be actual. As to the second, however, time, as a determination or order attaching to the things themselves, could not precede the objects as their condition and be cognized and intuited a priori through synthetic propositions. The latter, on the other hand, takes place very well if time is nothing but the subjective condition under which all intuitions can take place in us. For then this form of inner intuition can be represented before the objects, hence a priori."

The first of the conclusions from the concepts of § 4 and § 5, *Schlüsse aus diesen
Begriffen*, § 6 of the Transcendental Aesthetic's section on time (AA 3:59.19–30): four
sentences counted by full stop, the first's colon joining its two halves (the first 3:59.19–23,
its »denn« clause 3:59.22–23; the second 3:59.23–26; the third 3:59.26–28; the fourth
3:59.28–30). It parallels conclusion (a) on space, [KrV A 26 / B 42](../../p/krv-a26-b42/),
whose numeral »a)« that passage keeps, as this one does. The 1781 page A 32 carries the heading
*Schlüsse aus diesen Begriffen* without the section number, which the second edition added, and
ends inside the second sentence at »Was aber das« with the catchword »zweite«, the break between
two words; the rest is on A 33, which runs on into conclusion (b). The B page, 49, is given from
the standard concordance and is not verified. The AA line is the locator.

**The 1781 text, collated on wikisource's page-by-page transcription of the scan.** Three
wording variants: the 1781 edition reads »was vor sich selbst bestünde« where the Akademie text
(the second edition's) has »was für sich selbst bestände«, two words differing in the one phrase,
»vor« for »für« and the subjunctive »bestünde« for »bestände«; and »Diese letztere findet« for
»Dieses letztere findet«; and »anhangende« for »anhängende«, the umlaut-less form of the verb,
which the map reads as orthography and lists here so that the call is seen. Punctuation: in the second sentence a comma after »Gegenständen« and
after »vorhergehen«, »nicht vor den Gegenständen, als ihre Bedingung vorhergehen, und a priori«.
Orthography (»obiective«, »subiectiven«, »könte«, »betrift«, »erkant«, »statt
finden«, »kan«, »seyn«) is not listed. The transcription sets »a priori« in antiqua, as the Akademie text
sets it, and marks nothing as emphasized; the korpora HTML sets nothing in these lines in another
face.

**Translation.** The subjunctives of the first sentence are kept as "would": »bestände« "would
subsist", »anhinge« "would attach", »übrig bliebe« "would remain", »wäre« "would be"; »mithin«
"and so", the consequence drawn from either limb; »abstrahirt« is indicative, "abstracts", where
A 26 / B 42 has »abstrahirte«, "abstracted"; »derselben« after »Anschauung« is resolved as the
things, "the intuition of them"; »dennoch« "nevertheless"; »könnte« "could"; the second
sentence's »sie« is rendered by name, "time"; »Dieses letztere« is "the latter", which the map
reads as what the second sentence denied, preceding the objects as their condition and being
cognized a priori, said on the claims; »Bestimmung oder Ordnung« "determination or order";
»stattfinden« "take place", twice.

**What is held and what is not.** The first sentence's main clause is [c-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things](../../c/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-attaching-to-things/), the conclusion of
the map's argument [a-kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things](../../a/kant-time-not-subsisting-for-itself-nor-an-objective-determination-of-things/), set on the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) in the row on the division of appearance from thing in
itself and entered there under the premise that time is no determination of the thing in itself,
beside the premise on space; its »denn« clause is [c-kant-time-subsisting-for-itself-would-be-actual-without-an-actual-object](../../c/kant-time-subsisting-for-itself-would-be-actual-without-an-actual-object/), and the second sentence [c-kant-time-as-a-determination-attaching-to-things-themselves-could-not-precede-the-objects-and-be-cognized-a-priori](../../c/kant-time-as-a-determination-attaching-to-things-themselves-could-not-precede-the-objects-and-be-cognized-a-priori/), the
two grounds, each a premise of the argument and in no row. The third and fourth sentences are in
the passage and in no claim: the third is a conditional, that the latter takes place if time is
nothing but the subjective condition under which all intuitions can take place in us, whose
antecedent this passage does not assert, and the fourth gives its ground under the same »da«,
"then"; what the third supposes, that time is a subjective condition of intuition, the section
asserts two paragraphs on, in [KrV A 34–35 / B 51](../../p/krv-a34-35-b51-time-nothing-when-the-objects-are-taken-as-they-may-be-in-themselves/), held in a claim. Conclusion (b)
(3:59.31–60.12), that time is nothing other than the form of inner sense, and conclusion (c)
(3:60.13–27), that time is the formal condition a priori of all appearances, are in no passage.
