The second sentence of the passage, the first step of the reductio of JGB 15. »Dann« takes up
the supposition of the sentence before, »die Aussenwelt wäre das Werk unsrer Organe«, which
Nietzsche puts in the mouth of unnamed others (»Andere sagen gar«); the statement writes the
supposition out as the antecedent. The ground of the step is in the sentence, »als ein Stück
dieser Aussenwelt«. The first premise of [a-nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs](../../a/nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs/).
