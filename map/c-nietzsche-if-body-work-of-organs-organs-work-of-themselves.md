The third sentence of the passage, the second step of the reductio. »Dann« takes up the
sentence before, that our body would be the work of our organs; the step from the body to the
organs has no ground stated in the sentence, which the dash before »das Werk unsrer Organe«
marks as the point, and the map supplies none: that the organs are part of the body is not
written here, and the statement keeps the conditional as the text has it. The second premise of
[a-nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs](../../a/nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs/).
