The last two sentences of JGB 15. The conclusion is drawn by »Folglich« and ends with a dash and
a question mark, »-?«; the map keeps the supposition, »gesetzt, dass der Begriff causa sui etwas
gründlich Absurdes ist«, inside the statement, since the text makes the reductio depend on it,
and carries the closing mark into the statement itself. The map reads that mark as leaving
the conclusion put to the reader rather than withdrawn, and so treats the chain as an argument
Nietzsche makes; whether he makes it or stands away from it is a reading, the map's own, for
which it names no literature, and a dispute may contest it or ask for the two readings to be
set out as `r-` nodes. What the text asserts of the causa sui in its own
voice is in JGB 21, »der beste Selbst-Widerspruch, der bisher ausgedacht worden ist«
([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)); the map does not carry that assertion
into this claim, which stays under its supposition. Established by [a-nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs](../../a/nietzsche-external-world-not-work-of-organs/). The sentence
names no one; the »Andere« who say the external world is the work of our organs are not
identified, and no Kant or Schopenhauer statement in the map says it
([x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), the row on the subject's work).
