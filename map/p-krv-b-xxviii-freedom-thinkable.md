translation: "Now although I cannot cognize my soul, considered from the latter side, by any speculative reason (still less by empirical observation), and hence cannot cognize freedom either as a property of a being to which I ascribe effects in the world of sense, because I would have to cognize such a being as determined in its existence and yet not in time (which is impossible, because I can lay no intuition under my concept): I can nevertheless think freedom, that is, the representation of it at least contains no contradiction in itself, if our critical distinction of the two kinds of representation (the sensible and the intellectual) and the restriction of the pure concepts of the understanding arising from it, and hence also of the principles flowing from them, holds."

From the preface to the second edition. The span begins mid-line at »Ob ich nun gleich«
(3:17.28) and ends at »statt hat« (3:18.11). »Von der letzteren Seite« refers back to the
sentence before ([B xxvii–xxviii](../../p/krv-b-xxvii-xxviii-same-will-free/)), where the
will was taken »als einem Dinge an sich selbst angehörig«; the side is that of the thing in
itself. The comma after »Vernunft« before the parenthesis is the Korpus's. The A/B page is
given from the standard concordance; the AA line is the authoritative locator.
