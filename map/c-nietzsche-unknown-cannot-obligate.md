GD, »Wie die ›wahre Welt‹ endlich zur Fabel wurde«, stage 4: »wozu könnte uns etwas
Unbekanntes verpflichten?...« The statement is the map's reading of the rhetorical
question as a denial, and says so here; a reader may dispute the reading. The stage is
labelled by its parenthesis »Hahnenschrei des Positivismus«, and the statement is framed
as that stage's position, for the reason given on
[a-nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate](../../a/nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate/),
of which the claim is premise 3. The stage's »tröstend, erlösend« are not covered by the
question, which speaks only of »verpflichten«.
