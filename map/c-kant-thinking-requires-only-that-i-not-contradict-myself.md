The second sentence of Kant's footnote at B xxvi
([the note](../../p/krv-b-xxvi-footnote/)), the one the mark after »denken können« most
directly glosses, since it says what »denken« requires. The statement is the sentence whole
less its opening »Aber«, the contrast with the first sentence
([c-kant-cognizing-an-object-requires-proving-its-possibility](../../c/kant-cognizing-an-object-requires-proving-its-possibility/)),
and keeps the two »nur«, "only", each with the scope of its »wenn«, and the concession »ob ich zwar ... nicht stehen kann«,
"although I cannot vouch". »Was ich will« is the idiom, whatever I want, and the »will« in
it is the verb, not the noun »Wille« of B xxvii; the sentence says nothing of the will. It
names no concept: that it bears on the concept of a thing in itself is read from the place
of the mark, and the map says so on the comparison page where it is set. B xxviii's
»so kann ich mir doch die Freiheit denken, d. i. die Vorstellung davon enthält wenigstens
keinen Widerspruch in sich«
([c-kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds](../../c/kant-freedom-thinkable-if-critical-distinction-holds/))
is read by the map as saying of one concept, freedom, what this sentence says of thinking as
such: each has a »d. i.«, the note's equating not contradicting myself with my concept's
being a possible thought, B xxviii's equating the thinkability of freedom with its
representation's containing no contradiction; the map reads the one as the rule and the other
as its application, sets no link, since no connective joins the two and neither statement
draws the other, and says so on the comparison on the will. No attack link is set from JGB 16's »contradictio in adjecto«
([c-nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto](../../c/nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto/)):
that claim is set against the B xxvi sentence that applies thinking to »Dinge an sich
selbst«, and this sentence names no concept that could include a contradiction; a reader
may dispute the absence. JGB 21's »der beste Selbst-Widerspruch, der bisher ausgedacht
worden ist« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/))
says a self-contradiction was devised, not that it is a possible thought, and this sentence
names no concept; no attack link is set. Schopenhauer's § 19, »Außer dem Willen und der
Vorstellung ist uns gar nichts bekannt, noch denkbar«
([c-schopenhauer-nothing-known-but-will-and-representation](../../c/schopenhauer-nothing-known-but-will-and-representation/)),
and § 25's plurality, »nur in ihnen denkbar«, in time and space
([c-schopenhauer-plurality-conditioned-by-time-and-space-not-of-thing-in-itself](../../c/schopenhauer-plurality-conditioned-by-time-and-space-not-of-thing-in-itself/))
state what is thinkable and under what; whether their »denkbar« is this sentence's »denken«,
a concept's not contradicting itself, no passage in the map says, and the map does not
identify the two, so no attack and no shared premise is set; a reader may dispute the
absence.
