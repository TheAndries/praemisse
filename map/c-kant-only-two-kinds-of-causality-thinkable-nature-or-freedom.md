The first sentence of section III of the resolution of the antinomy (AA 3:362.29–30), asserted, »Man kann sich nur ... denken«, of what can be thought; »nur« is kept. The sentence names freedom as one of the two kinds of causality and says nothing of the will; the second and third sentences of the passage, which define causality according to nature, are in the passage and in no claim, and the fourth defines freedom in the cosmological sense ([c-kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-the-faculty-of-beginning-a-state-of-itself](../../c/kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-the-faculty-of-beginning-a-state-of-itself/)). No link is set. No Schopenhauer statement in the map divides causality into two kinds: § 55 has the will as thing in itself know »keine Nothwendigkeit« ([c-schopenhauer-will-not-subject-to-principle-of-sufficient-reason-knows-no-necessity-is-free](../../c/schopenhauer-will-not-subject-to-principle-of-sufficient-reason-knows-no-necessity-is-free/)) and every thing »als Erscheinung ... nothwendig« and in itself will and free ([c-schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free](../../c/schopenhauer-every-thing-as-appearance-necessary-in-itself-will-and-free/)), which say of what is free and what necessary, not of two causalities. No Nietzsche statement in the map says how many kinds of causality can be thought; JGB 21 has cause and effect as »reine Begriffe« and »conventionelle Fiktionen« ([c-nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions](../../c/nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions/)), a sentence on what the concepts are, not a denial that two kinds can be thought; JGB 36's attempt to posit the will's causality »als die einzige« ([c-nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only](../../c/nietzsche-will-causality-posited-as-the-only/)) stands under »Gesetzt« and is a proposal about one kind, not a count of what can be thought; no link is set. Set on the comparison on the will in the row on freedom, since 2026-10-27, for the word »Freiheit«, as every sentence of the three spans that names it is, and spoken of under *On freedom* there as the frame of the definitions that follow it; the row's subject is the will, which the sentence does not name, and the note says so.
