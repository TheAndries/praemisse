The fourth sentence of the section (AA 3:366.19–22), asserted outright, »widerspricht
keinem«. »Eine solche doppelte Seite« is the one the sentence before gave the causality of a
being, intelligible and sensible
([c-kant-causality-of-a-being-considered-from-two-sides](../../c/kant-causality-of-a-being-considered-from-two-sides/));
the statement names the two sides so that it can be read alone. What is asserted is a
non-contradiction: the thought is consistent with every concept »die wir uns von
Erscheinungen und von einer möglichen Erfahrung zu machen haben«, and nothing more; the
sentence does not say that any object has the double side. The ground follows at once,
»Denn«, and is the map's checked argument
[a-kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience](../../a/kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience/),
whose premises are the next sentence's two clauses
([c-kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances](../../c/kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances/),
[c-kant-nothing-hinders-attributing-causality-to-transcendental-object](../../c/kant-nothing-hinders-attributing-causality-to-transcendental-object/))
and a bridge the map names. The non-contradiction here is of a thought with the concepts of
experience; the non-contradiction at B xxvii–xxviii, »ohne daß hiebei ein Widerspruch
vorgeht«, is between two predicates of one will
([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)),
and the map does not identify the two.
