The main clause of the fifth sentence of the section (AA 3:366.24–27), drawn by »da ..., so«
from the clause before it
([c-kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances](../../c/kant-transcendental-object-must-underlie-appearances/));
the statement keeps the »since« so that it can be read alone. What is asserted is permissive,
»hindert nichts«: nothing stands in the way of the attribution; the sentence does not say
that the transcendental object has such a causality. The causality attributed is »nicht
Erscheinung«, its effect is »dennoch in der Erscheinung«, and it comes »außer der Eigenschaft,
dadurch er erscheint«, as a second thing attributed to the one object. The »nicht« after
»daß« belongs to the construction »hindern, daß ... nicht« and is not a denial. It is a
premise of the map's checked argument
[a-kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience](../../a/kant-double-side-contradicts-no-concept-of-experience/),
where the form takes its »da ..., so« as the step from the transcendental object's underlying
appearances to the attribution's being unhindered, and the argument's body says so.
