The fifth sentence of section 7, counting its title line, whole; the text's colon is
kept, and the impersonal »Man hat ... entkleidet« is rendered as a passive in the
statement, "has been stripped", and as "One has stripped" in the quotation, named here.
The sentence names the will twice, »auf Wille« and »die Lehre vom Willen«; what it says of
the doctrine is its purpose, »zum Zweck der Strafe«, and it does not say what the will is
or whether there is one. The sentence before it, on the instinct of wanting to punish and
to judge, and the one after, on the priests who wanted a right to punish, are in the
passage and in no claim. The comparison [x-the-will](../../x/the-will/) sets the sentence
in its row on whether the will is free, since 2026-10-24, with the section's first sentence
([c-nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility](../../c/nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility/)): it is set for its tracing of being to »Wille ... Akte der Verantwortlichkeit«, the
tracing the section's title, »Irrthum vom freien Willen«, calls the error of free will,
and its first sentence ties to »"verantwortlich"«; the map does not set it in
the row on what is drawn from the will, since the purpose of a doctrine is not an
inference drawn from the will, said in that row's paragraph. GD »Die vier grossen
Irrthümer« 3 has »man wäre ja sonst zu ihr nicht frei, für sie nicht verantwortlich
gewesen« among the beliefs it recounts
([p-gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause](../../p/gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause/)), a sentence
in that passage and in no claim; no link joins the two sections, which the text does not
join. No attack link is set on any Kant or Schopenhauer statement: none in the map states
what the doctrine of the will was invented for, and this sentence denies no statement
about the will itself. JGB 19's »Wille und Aktion« and JGB 21's »"unfreie Wille"« are the
nearest Nietzsche statements on the will and its actions
([c-nietzsche-belief-will-and-action-one-an-erroneous-inference](../../c/nietzsche-belief-will-and-action-one-an-erroneous-inference/),
[c-nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology](../../c/nietzsche-unfree-will-is-mythology/)); this sentence is joined to neither by the
