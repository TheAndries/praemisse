The second sentence of the passage (AA 3:211.18–22; A 254 / B 310), asserted outright. Its
subject is the concept of a noumenon, which the sentence itself defines in apposition, »d. i.
eines Dinges, welches gar nicht als Gegenstand der Sinne, sondern als ein Ding an sich selbst
(lediglich durch einen reinen Verstand) gedacht werden soll«, and the statement keeps the definition, with its »soll«, "is to be thought", its parenthesis turned into commas, the change named here; the ground, after »denn«,
is kept with its »doch«, "after all", dropped from the statement and named here. The sentence
says what the concept is not, contradictory, and gives one ground, that sensibility cannot be
asserted to be the only possible kind of intuition; it does not say that such a thing exists or
is cognized, which the fourth sentence of the same passage denies for us
([c-kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us](../../c/kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us/)).

The preface's sentence that we must at least be able to think the same objects as things in
themselves, »wenn gleich nicht erkennen«
([c-kant-things-in-themselves-thinkable-not-knowable](../../c/kant-things-in-themselves-thinkable-not-knowable/)),
says that the thing in itself must be thinkable; this sentence says that the concept of a thing
so thought contains no contradiction, which the footnote at B xxvi makes the condition of
thinking, »wenn ich mir nur nicht selbst widerspreche«
([c-kant-thinking-requires-only-that-i-not-contradict-myself](../../c/kant-thinking-requires-only-that-i-not-contradict-myself/)).
The three places say what each other needs, and no connective joins them across the two texts,
so the map sets no `depends_on` among them and says that the fit is its reading.

One attack is set. Nietzsche's JGB 16, that »"unmittelbare Gewissheit", ebenso wie "absolute
Erkenntniss" und "Ding an sich", eine contradictio in adjecto in sich schliesst«
([c-nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto](../../c/nietzsche-immediate-certainty-contradictio-in-adjecto/)),
denies in its words what this sentence states in its own: that the concept of a thing thought
as a thing in itself is »gar nicht widersprechend«. The same Nietzsche claim is set since
2026-10-09 as an attack on the preface's sentence above, where the denial reaches the target
through the step that what includes a contradiction cannot be consistently thought; here the
target states the absence of contradiction itself, and the attacker's words meet it without a
step. No sentence of JGB 16 names Kant or a passage of his; the link rests on »Ding an sich«
and »contradictio in adjecto« against »Ding an sich selbst« and »gar nicht widersprechend«, and
the map says so. No Schopenhauer statement in the map says of the concept of a thing in itself
or of a noumenon that it is or is not contradictory, so no link is set from any; § 19's »noch
denkbar« and § 25's »nur in ihnen denkbar« say what is thinkable of the will and of plurality,
not whether a concept contradicts itself. Each absence is open to dispute.
