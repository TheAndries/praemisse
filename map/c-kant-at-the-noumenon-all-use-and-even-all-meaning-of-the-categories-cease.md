The first sentence of the passage (AA 3:210.12–18; B 308), asserted outright: the main clause, »da
hört der ganze Gebrauch, ja selbst alle Bedeutung der Kategorien völlig auf«, and its ground, the
»denn« clause, that »selbst die Möglichkeit der Dinge, die den Kategorien entsprechen sollen, läßt
sich gar nicht einsehen«, both indicative. The statement keeps the two and drops the sentence's
»weshalb« clause, Kant's appeal to what he adduced »in der allgemeinen Anmerkung zum vorigen
Hauptstücke gleich zu Anfang«, the *Allgemeine Anmerkung zum System der Grundsätze* (AA 3:198.13 ff.; B 288–294 by the standard concordance, not verified), a text in no passage in the map; the clause says where a ground is to be found and asserts nothing
further of the noumenon, and the drop is named here. »diese Zeiteinheit« points back to the sentence before this one, the last of [KrV B 307–308](../../p/krv-b307/), where the categories »nur in Beziehung auf die Einheit der Anschauungen in Raum und Zeit Bedeutung haben«, a clause in that passage and in the statement of no claim; the statement drops the demonstrative, so that it reads alone and asserts nothing of that clause, and keeps "the unity of time", the resolution standing here and on the passage as the map's. That the sentence says »Zeiteinheit« where the sentence before names space and time is the text's. The correlative »da«, "there", that answers the »Wo« is dropped from the statement, and the drop is named here. »beim Noumenon« is "in the case of the
noumenon"; the sentence before speaks of »Noumenen im negativen Verstande«, and whether this
sentence's noumenon is the noumenon in that sense the sentence does not say; the statement keeps
"the noumenon", and the map identifies nothing. »ja selbst« "indeed even"; »völlig« "entirely";
»denn selbst« "for even"; »sollen« "are to"; »läßt sich gar nicht einsehen« "is not at all to be
seen", »einsehen« "see" as at B 307 and B 310. The sentence names the noumenon by the chapter's word
and says of it what the row's criterion asks, what cannot be said of it: that the categories have no
use and no meaning in its case, and that even the possibility of things that are to correspond to
them is not to be seen.
