Prolegomena §57, first paragraph (AA 4:350.21–26). Kant's "denn" (4:350.27) gives the
ground: space, time and all concepts of the understanding have no use but to make
experience possible (4:350.27–33), which the map holds as a separate claim this one depends
on. It restates from the Prolegomena what B xxvi holds as [the restriction of speculative cognition to objects of experience](../../c/kant-knowledge-limited-to-experience/). It is the "Ungereimtheit" on one side; the next paragraph names "eine noch größere
Ungereimtheit" on the other (4:350.34).

Set on the [comparison on the thing in itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/), in the row on what can be said of the thing
in itself, since 2026-11-01, for what the sentence says cannot be claimed of a thing assumed not to
be an object of possible experience, the least cognition determining what it is in itself, under
»würde ... sein«, the absurdity asserted of the hope; until then the claim sat in no row of either
comparison, and it was set there by the criterion that set the chapter's sentences at B 310–315 in
the same row, applied to every Kant statement already on the page. Its quote block was added the
same day. No link is added: the chapter's sentence at B 314 that what objects may be as objects of the
pure understanding remains unknown ([c-kant-what-objects-may-be-as-objects-of-the-pure-understanding-remains-unknown](../../c/kant-what-objects-may-be-as-objects-of-the-pure-understanding-remains-unknown/)) is the other Kant
statement in the map with »möglichen Erfahrung« and the thing in itself, and no connective joins the
two works.
