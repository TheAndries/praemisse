GD, »Wie die ›wahre Welt‹ endlich zur Fabel wurde«, stage 4: »Die wahre Welt -
unerreichbar? jedenfalls unerreicht.« The parenthesis assigns the stage to positivism
(»Hahnenschrei des Positivismus«), and the statement is framed as that stage's position,
for the reason given on the argument. The claim is premise 1 of
[a-nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate](../../a/nietzsche-true-world-does-not-obligate/); the question mark on »unerreichbar« is
kept, since the stage leaves attainability open and asserts only that it is unattained.
