The sixth sentence of the passage. Its »dieser Gränze« points back to the sentence before it, »Sie begränzen sich unmittelbar: wo das Objekt anfängt, hört das Subjekt auf«, and the statement restates that antecedent, "the boundary at which subject and object immediately bound each other", so that it reads alone; the restatement is the map's resolution of the pronoun. The sentence asserts its »daß« clause as what the commonality »zeigt sich eben darin«, and the statement keeps the frame and the clause's »auch ohne«, »vollständig« and »d.h. in Kants Sprache«. The link `shares_premise_with` is set to Kant's A 24 / B 38 ([c-kant-space-a-priori](../../c/kant-space-a-priori/)) on the premise that space is given a priori: Kant's statement has space as »eine nothwendige Vorstellung a priori«, this one has the forms of every object, space among them, »a priori in unserm Bewußtseyn liegen«, in Kant's language by Schopenhauer's own words, and the comparison [x-thing-in-itself](../../x/thing-in-itself/) names the pair in a third entry under that premise, beside the two from the Anhang. What the sentence adds, that the forms are found »vom Subjekt ausgehend« even without cognition of the object, is Schopenhauer's and not in Kant's sentence, and the premise does not cover it. No link is set to the claim that space is the subjective condition of sensibility ([c-kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility](../../c/kant-space-subjective-condition-of-sensibility/)): that statement words the form's being the subject's, and this sentence says the forms can be found »vom Subjekt ausgehend«, not that they are the subject's, so neither asserts the other's proposition. The sentence calls the forms »Formen alles Objekts«; § 24's first sentence says the same three forms can be found as well from the subject as from the object and be named with equal right the subject's ways of intuiting or qualities of the object ([c-schopenhauer-forms-in-consciousness-independent-of-objects](../../c/schopenhauer-forms-in-consciousness-independent-of-objects/)), and the map draws no link between the two sentences, which no connective joins. The next sentence, which credits Kant with the discovery, is [c-schopenhauer-discovery-of-the-forms-a-priori-a-chief-merit-of-kant](../../c/schopenhauer-discovery-of-the-forms-a-priori-a-chief-merit-of-kant/).
