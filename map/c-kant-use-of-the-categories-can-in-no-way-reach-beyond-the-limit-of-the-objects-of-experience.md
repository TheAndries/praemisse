The main clause of the one sentence of the passage (AA 3:210.26–27; B 308–309), »so kann auch der
Gebrauch der Kategorien keinesweges über die Grenze der Gegenstände der Erfahrung hinausreichen«,
asserted outright, drawn by »Da nun ..., so« from the sentence's ground clause, that the intellectual
intuition lies absolutely outside our faculty of cognition
([c-kant-intellectual-intuition-lies-absolutely-outside-our-faculty-of-cognition](../../c/kant-intellectual-intuition-lies-absolutely-outside-our-faculty-of-cognition/)).
The step is the map's argument
[a-kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience](../../a/kant-use-of-the-categories-cannot-reach-beyond-the-limit-of-the-objects-of-experience/),
named in `depends_on`, which reads the »so« as drawing also from the sentence before, the conditional
that applying the categories to objects not regarded as appearances would require an intuition other
than the sensible
([c-kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense](../../c/kant-applying-the-categories-to-objects-not-regarded-as-appearances-would-require-another-intuition-and-the-object-would-be-a-noumenon-in-the-positive-sense/)),
to which the ground clause's »eine solche« points, and which names the two bridges it needs as the
map's. »auch«, "too", is dropped from the statement, so that it reads alone, and named here; what the »auch« sets this clause beside the text does not say, and the map reads it as the restriction the paragraph has already stated of the categories, B 308's first sentence, »da hört der ganze Gebrauch ... der Kategorien völlig auf« (3:210.13–14), the other candidate being B 307–308's »von seinen Kategorien ... keinen Gebrauch machen könne« (3:210.08–09), the reading the map's and open to dispute; the »so« with its ground clause is dropped from the statement too, where the main-clause claim of A 538's »da ..., so« sentence of 2026-10-19 kept its "Since", because the step is here an argument and the ground its own claim; the passage's translation, which did not
render it, renders it since 2026-11-04. »keinesweges« "in no way"; »über die Grenze ... hinausreichen«
"reach beyond the limit", »Grenze« "limit", as against »Schranke«, throughout the map; »der
Gegenstände der Erfahrung« "of the objects of experience". The clause names neither the thing in
itself nor a noumenon.

The sentence goes on: »und den Sinnenwesen correspondiren zwar freilich Verstandeswesen, auch mag es
Verstandeswesen geben, auf welche unser sinnliches Anschauungsvermögen gar keine Beziehung hat, aber
unsere Verstandesbegriffe, als bloße Gedankenformen für unsere sinnliche Anschauung, reichen nicht im
mindesten auf diese hinaus« (3:210.28–32), clauses in the passage and in the statement of no claim,
and closes »was also von uns Noumenon genannt wird, muß als ein solches nur in negativer Bedeutung
verstanden werden«, the clauses between being three by the map's count, the sentence of [c-kant-noumenon-negative-only](../../c/kant-noumenon-negative-only/),
which `depends_on` this claim by that »also« (3:210.32), named in `supports`; that the »also« reaches
back over the clauses between to this one is the map's reading, said on that claim.

The nearest sentences in the map are these, and no link is set from any. B xxvi's sentence that all
possible speculative cognition of reason is restricted to mere objects of experience
([c-kant-knowledge-limited-to-experience](../../c/kant-knowledge-limited-to-experience/)), which has
cited this passage for this clause since 2026-10-06 as a parallel restriction, says of speculative
cognition of reason what this clause says of the use of the categories; that the two are one
restriction is the map's reading, no connective joins the preface to the chapter, and no link is set.
