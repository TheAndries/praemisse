The third sentence of the passage (AA 3:211.22–27), the one that straddles the 1781 edition's
page break, A 254–255 / B 310, asserted outright. »Ferner« is dropped from the statement and
named here; »dieser Begriff«, the concept of a noumenon of the sentence before
([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-not-contradictory](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-not-contradictory/)),
is resolved by name; the parenthesis is kept, its »denn« rendered by the semicolon, its »jene«
resolved as the passage says, to sensible cognition; »bis über ... auszudehnen« is "extend
over", »einschränken« "restrict", »Gebiet« "domain". The sentence says why the concept is
necessary, in two »um ... zu« clauses, the second drawn from the first by »also«, and the
statement keeps both and the "thus". What the necessity is for is a restriction of sensible
cognition, not a cognition of the thing in itself, and the fourth sentence says that nothing
is given beyond sensibility
([c-kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us](../../c/kant-possibility-of-noumena-not-to-be-seen-range-outside-appearances-empty-for-us/)).

No premise is shared and no attack is set. The premise the page already enters between A 236
/ B 295 and the Anhang (ZA II 516), that the laws that govern experience are not to be applied
beyond it, is the nearest: this sentence restricts the objective validity of sensible cognition
to short of things in themselves, and Schopenhauer's »Er zeigte, daß die Gesetze ... nicht
anzuwenden sind« speaks of laws, not of intuition or its validity; the two are not one
proposition, and the sentence is not entered under that premise, a reading open to dispute.
Nietzsche's GD, that the "true" world's concept is one of which nothing is known and which
obligates nothing (Fabel, stages 4 and 5), and JGB 21's »"An-sich"« say nothing of a concept
being necessary to restrict sensibility, and no Nietzsche statement in the map denies that it
is; no Schopenhauer statement says a concept of the thing in itself is necessary or not. Each
absence is open to dispute.

Set on the comparison on the thing in itself, in the row on what can be said of the thing in
itself, since 2026-10-30, for what the sentence says the concept of a noumenon is for.
