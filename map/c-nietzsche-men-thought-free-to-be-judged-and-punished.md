The seventh sentence of section 7, counting its title line, whole, with its
parenthesis; the parenthesis's dash and its closing dots are dropped from the statement
and its »womit« rendered "whereby", and the dash before »um schuldig« is rendered as a
comma, all named here; »"frei"« keeps Nietzsche's quotation marks. The sentence has a
»folglich« inside it: from men's having been thought free in order to be judged it draws
that every action had to be thought as willed and as originating in consciousness. The
step is inside one sentence and the map formalizes no argument from it and sets no
`depends_on`, which a reader may ask for; the step, as the map reads it, runs from the
purpose of the thought to what had to be thought, not from a premise to what follows from
its truth. »gedacht« is "thought", the word the map uses for Kant's »gedacht« at
B xxvii–xxviii, where the same will »wird ... gedacht« as not free in the appearance and
as free as belonging to a thing in itself
([c-kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself](../../c/kant-same-will-not-free-as-appearance-free-as-thing-in-itself/)); that
Nietzsche's »"frei" gedacht« and Kant's »als frei« »gedacht« say one thing no passage in
the map says, and the map does not say it; no attack link is set, since this sentence
says why men were thought free and denies no statement that a will is or is thought free.
The comparison [x-the-will](../../x/the-will/) sets the sentence in its row on whether
the will is free, since 2026-10-24, with [c-nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility](../../c/nietzsche-free-will-a-theologians-artifice-for-responsibility/) and [c-nietzsche-doctrine-of-the-will-invented-for-punishment](../../c/nietzsche-doctrine-of-the-will-invented-for-punishment/); the sense of »frei« is
responsibility before a judge, as the row's note and the paragraph on freedom say. GD
»Die vier grossen Irrthümer« 3 says of the belief that an action's causes are to be sought
in consciousness, as motives, »man wäre ja sonst zu ihr nicht frei, für sie nicht
verantwortlich gewesen«
([p-gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause](../../p/gd-irrthuemer-3-will-as-cause/)), the same
pairing of freedom, responsibility and the origin in consciousness, in that passage and in
no claim; the two sections are joined by no connective, and no link is set.
