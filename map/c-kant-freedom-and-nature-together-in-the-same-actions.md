The last sentence of the section (AA 3:368.10–13), subjunctive, »würde ... angetroffen
werden«, kept as »would«. It closes the fourth paragraph and is drawn from the two paragraphs before it by »So würde denn«, »Thus«,
and the map sets `depends_on` on the two sentences it sums, the subject under its empirical
character, whose actions flow from nature
([c-kant-actions-explicable-by-laws-of-nature-by-empirical-character](../../c/kant-actions-explicable-by-laws-of-nature-by-empirical-character/)),
and under its intelligible character, free of natural necessity
([c-kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character](../../c/kant-active-being-free-of-natural-necessity-by-intelligible-character/)),
quoting the connective and not formalizing the step; the sentence between them, on how the
being's effects begin
([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)),
is part of what »So« gathers and is not set as a dependency, since the conclusion names
only the two causes. »Widerstreit« is »conflict«, not the »Widerspruch« of B xxvii–xxviii,
and the sentence does not say that the two are without contradiction; it says they are found
together at the same actions. »nachdem man sie ... vergleicht« is »according as one compares
them«, »nachdem« in the sense of »je nachdem«. This is the sentence of the section nearest to B
xxviii–xxix's »eben derselben Handlung (in anderer Beziehung genommen)«
([c-kant-same-action-in-another-relation](../../c/kant-same-action-in-another-relation/)),
and the comparison on the will sets the two side by side without identifying »Beziehung«
and the comparison with a cause.
