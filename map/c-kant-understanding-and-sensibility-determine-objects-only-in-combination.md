The third and fourth sentences of the passage (AA 3:213.32–36; A 258 / B 314), the close of the
paragraph, both asserted outright and joined into one statement by the semicolon; the fourth is a
conditional whose antecedent, »Wenn wir sie trennen«, names a case and not a saying, and the
statement keeps it conditional. »bei uns« is "in our case"; »bestimmen« "determine"; »beziehen
auf« "relate to"; »in beiden Fällen aber« "but in both cases", »aber« kept. The two sentences
name neither the thing in itself nor a noumenon: they say of understanding and sensibility how
they determine objects and what their separation leaves.

No link is set. The two sentences follow, with no connective, the sentence that what objects may
be as objects of the pure understanding remains unknown
([c-kant-what-objects-may-be-as-objects-of-the-pure-understanding-remains-unknown](../../c/kant-what-objects-may-be-as-objects-of-the-pure-understanding-remains-unknown/)),
and the chapter's sentences on a noumenon in the map, that the concept of a noumenon is only of
negative use, since no intuition is given for it
([c-kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use](../../c/kant-concept-of-a-noumenon-merely-a-limiting-concept-of-negative-use/))
and that the understanding sets itself the limit of cognizing things in themselves through no
category ([c-kant-understanding-calls-things-in-themselves-noumena-and-thinks-them-only-as-an-unknown-something](../../c/kant-understanding-calls-things-in-themselves-noumena-and-thinks-them-only-as-an-unknown-something/)),
say of one concept what these two sentences say of concepts without intuitions in general; that
the general sentence is the ground of the particular ones is the map's reading, drawn by no
connective, and no link is set. No Schopenhauer or Nietzsche statement in the map speaks of
understanding and sensibility, or of concepts and intuitions, in combination or apart: the
nearest Schopenhauer statement, § 19's, that the body is given »als Vorstellung in verständiger
Anschauung« and as will ([c-schopenhauer-body-given-in-two-ways](../../c/schopenhauer-body-given-in-two-ways/)),
names an intuition and the understanding and says nothing of concepts; the Anhang's critique of
Kant's doctrine of the categories is in no passage, said in the page's list of what the map does
not yet contain; and the one Nietzsche statement in the map with »Begriffe«, JGB 21's, that one should use cause and
effect »nur als reiner Begriffe« ([c-nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions](../../c/nietzsche-cause-and-effect-conventional-fictions/)),
speaks of two concepts as fictions and not of concepts without intuitions, so no link is set.
Each absence is open to dispute.
