The second and third sentences of the section's second paragraph (AA 3:367.08–15), both
subjunctive, »würde ... entstehen«, »würde ... unterworfen sein«, »stände«, kept as »would«.
The second sentence draws by »mithin« from the first clause to the second, and the third sums
up, »Mit einem Worte«; the statement keeps both connectives. The law named is given in the
subjunctive of indirect statement, »antreffe«, and is rendered in the indicative as the law's
content. »In ihm« and »desselben« bind to »Dieses handelnde Subject« of the sentence before
([c-kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time](../../c/kant-intelligible-character-under-no-conditions-of-time/)),
and the statement names it and the character under which this holds, since the sentence
begins with an anaphor. »so fern sie intellectuell ist« is a restriction: the same subject's causality, insofar as it is empirical, would stand in the series
([c-kant-actions-explicable-by-laws-of-nature-by-empirical-character](../../c/kant-actions-explicable-by-laws-of-nature-by-empirical-character/)).
