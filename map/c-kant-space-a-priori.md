The first half is the second point of the metaphysical exposition of space (A 24 / B 38–39,
AA 3:52.30–53.04); its ground there, that one can never represent that there is no space
though one can think that no objects are found in it (AA 3:52.31–33), is not formalized
here. The second half is the third point and its conclusion: space is a pure intuition, not
a discursive concept (AA 3:53.05–06), and an a priori, non-empirical intuition underlies all
concepts of it (AA 3:53.14–16).
