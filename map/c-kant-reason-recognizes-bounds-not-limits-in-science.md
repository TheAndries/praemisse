Prolegomena §57, AA 4:352.29–33. That metaphysics, by contrast, leads us to limits (4:353.16–18) is [its own claim](../../c/kant-metaphysics-leads-to-limits/). The
distinction is Kant's definition at 4:352.21–25: limits (*Grenzen*) presuppose a space
outside that encloses a place; bounds (*Schranken*) are mere negations affecting a
magnitude that lacks absolute completeness.
