The sixth sentence of the passage (AA 3:363.19–24). The main clause is asserted, »so schafft sich die Vernunft die Idee«; the idea's content stands in the subjunctive, »könne«, »dürfe«, kept as "could" and "having to", so that what the sentence asserts is that reason makes the idea, not that any such spontaneity is. »auf solche Weise« points to the law of the sentence before, that everything that happens has a cause ([c-kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-a-pure-transcendental-idea](../../c/kant-freedom-in-the-cosmological-sense-a-pure-transcendental-idea/)), restated in the statement so that it reads alone; the »Da« gives the ground inside the sentence and no `depends_on` is set, and the »aber«, which sets the sentence against the law of the one before, is dropped from the statement and named here. »von selbst anheben« is "begin of itself", the words of A 541's subject that would begin its effects »von selbst« ([c-kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself](../../c/kant-subject-begins-its-effects-of-itself/)), under »würde« there and inside an idea here. Nietzsche's JGB 21 says that the desire for »"Freiheit des Willens"« in the metaphysical superlative sense is »eben jene causa sui zu sein« and to pull oneself by the hair out of the swamp of nothingness ([c-nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui](../../c/nietzsche-desire-for-freedom-of-the-will-is-to-be-causa-sui/)), and that the causa sui is »der beste Selbst-Widerspruch, der bisher ausgedacht worden ist« ([c-nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction](../../c/nietzsche-causa-sui-best-self-contradiction/)): this sentence has reason create the idea of a spontaneity that begins to act without a prior cause, and whether that spontaneity is Nietzsche's causa sui, a cause of itself, no passage in the map says; the identification would be the map's, the denial would need Nietzsche's two sentences joined and would hit the idea's content, which Kant does not assert, and no attack is set, said on JGB 21's nodes as well.
