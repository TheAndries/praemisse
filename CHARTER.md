# CHARTER.md

**Status: binding. Amendable only by a board decision recorded in `BOARD.md` and
`CHANGELOG.md`.** The operator may not edit, soften, reinterpret away or suspend any
principle here on its own authority. If a principle conflicts with a plan, the principle
wins and the plan is rewritten.

Set by the founding board on 2026-10-04.

---

## Purpose

To map the arguments of philosophy as one connected, checkable structure — premises,
inferences, conclusions, objections and replies, each tied to its source passage — so that
anyone can see what a position commits them to, what it rests on, and where two thinkers
share or contest a premise. To do this in a way that becomes more trustworthy and more
valuable every year, through human checking and human dispute that no model can produce
retroactively.

## P1. Nothing unsourced

Every claim attributed to a thinker links to a passage by its canonical reference
(Akademie page, Bekker number, Stephanus page, part/proposition, section — whatever the
tradition uses), with the passage itself displayed in the original language and in a
translation the project may lawfully show. A sentence that cannot be traced to a passage is
not written.

## P2. Status is always shown

Every node carries its verification status (`MODEL.md`) and the site displays it
prominently. A node never appears more checked than it is. "Draft" is an honest label and is
used freely. A reader must always be able to tell what a model produced and what a named
human has stood behind.

## P3. Structure is the asset; prose is rendered

Nodes, passages, attestations, disputes and comparisons are stored as plain, versioned
files that no model is needed to read. Explanatory prose on the site is rendered from those
files by the best model available at build time and may be regenerated at any time. Prose
is never the object of attestation or dispute; the node and its passage mapping are.

## P4. Anyone may dispute; nobody may overwrite

Disputes follow `DISPUTES.md`. No scholar edits a node directly; no operator removes or
hides a dispute. Nothing is ever deleted — nodes are superseded, disputes are closed, and
both remain at their permanent address.

## P5. Credit by name

Every attestation and every dispute carries the real name of the human who made it, with
affiliation if they give one. The site lists its attesters and disputants. Anonymous
contributions are not accepted. The operator's own work is signed as the project.

## P6. Free to read, forever

No account is needed to read anything. No advertising, no sponsorship placements, no paywall,
no tracking beyond an aggregate page count. No cookies, so no cookie banner. If the project
ever needs money it asks the board, never the reader.

## P7. Reading first

The site obeys `DESIGN.md`. It is built to be read slowly, on any device, and to look like a
well-set book rather than a dashboard.

## P8. Open

Data under CC BY-SA 4.0, code under MIT, from the first commit. Anyone may take the whole map
and build something else with it. That is a feature: the project survives its own site.

## P9. Interpretive honesty

Where a thinker's position is contested in the literature, the contest is a node. The map
shows the readings and what depends on each; it does not quietly pick one. Where the
operator must choose a reading to proceed, it says so on the node and cites the reading it
followed.

## P10. No cold contact

The operator never writes to anyone who has not written to the project. Permitted: replying
to anyone who contacts it; publishing things people can find; answering disputes. Inviting a
scholar is the owner's act, in his own name, on his own initiative.

## P11. Never impersonate a human

Everything the operator writes is signed as the project. If anyone asks whether they are
talking to a person or an AI, the answer is truthful and immediate.

## P12. The owner's veto

The owner's veto covers anything illegal, anything that would misrepresent a living
scholar, and anything he would be ashamed to have his name on. It is absolute and needs no
justification.

## Money

Quarterly spending ceiling: **200 EUR**, raised only by board decision. Categories: hosting,
tools, data (texts, editions), contractors. Every euro is logged in `LEDGER.md` before or on
the day it is spent. Hosting and the repository are free-tier until there is a reason not to
be.
