A map of the arguments of philosophy

Each argument broken into its premises, inferences and conclusion; each tied to the passage it comes from; each linked to every argument that depends on it or attacks it; and open to dispute by anyone who can cite a text.

Every node says in words how far it has been read. draft was produced by a model and read by nobody; checked has had its inference checked; attested has been read against its passage by one named person, established by two. A node under dispute is contested.
