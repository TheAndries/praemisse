praemisse

A map of the arguments of philosophy, broken into explicit premises, inferences and conclusions, each tied to the passage it comes from, each linked to every argument that depends on it or attacks it, and open to dispute by anyone who can cite a text.

Every node shows its status in words: draft was produced by a model and read by nobody; checked has had its inference checked by machine; attested has been read against its passage by one named human; established by two. A node under dispute is marked contested.
