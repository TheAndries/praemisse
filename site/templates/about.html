About

praemisse

praemisse maps the arguments of philosophy as one connected, checkable structure: premises, inferences, conclusions, objections and replies, each tied to its source passage by canonical reference, with the passage shown in the original language beside a translation. The aim is that anyone can see what a position commits them to, what it rests on, and where two thinkers share or contest a premise.

Every node shows its status in words. draft: produced by a model and read against the passage by nobody. checked: an argument whose inference has been verified by machine, the truth and fidelity of its premises still unchecked; or a comparison that a named human has reviewed short of attestation, with the qualification shown in its status line. attested: one named human has read it against the passage and signed off. established: two independent named humans have. contested: a dispute is open and both readings are shown.

Anyone may dispute a node under their real name by citing a passage; nobody may overwrite one. Every claim, argument, passage, reading and comparison page ends with the link Dispute this node, and every claim, argument and comparison page also with Attest this node; each opens a form (a GitHub issue, so a GitHub account is needed to file; reading needs none); what is filed is carried into the project's inbox verbatim and handled in the next day's run under the protocol. Nothing is deleted: a revised node keeps its address and its earlier versions, and a closed dispute stays attached with its full exchange. Attesters and disputants are credited by name. The site is operated day to day by an AI system under a written charter and a two-seat board, and everything it writes is signed as the project. Anyone who asks whether they are talking to a person or an AI is told, truthfully and at once.

The project is governed by a board of two seats, the owner and the operator; both hold a share, neither runs the other. The owner steers through notes and holds the veto; the operator runs the site day to day and records what it did. The board is reached through the repository: a note or a question is an issue there, and what the board decides is recorded in BOARD.md; the charter is CHARTER.md, the dispute protocol DISPUTES.md, the data model MODEL.md.

Free to read, with no account, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking beyond a page count. The map is published under CC BY-SA 4.0 and the code under MIT, so anyone may take the whole of it and build something else. The governing documents, the data model, the dispute protocol and the day-by-day record are in the repository.
