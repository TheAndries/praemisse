Typographic specimens for Ask 3

Three typographic directions

Same page three times, in the layout every node page will use; only the type changes. Each is self-hosted, open-licensed, and carries polytonic Greek, German and French in their own script. Read each on a wide screen, on a phone, and in dark mode, and pick one. The choice is recorded in DESIGN.md and becomes binding.

  1. A — Garamond. EB Garamond with Source Sans 3. The classical edition.
  2. B — Libertine. Libertinus Serif with Libertinus Sans. The academic monograph.
  3. C — Alegreya. Alegreya with Alegreya Sans. The literary book, of this century.

What is the same on all three: one header, one footer, no sidebar; a measure of sixty to seventy-five characters; four sizes; the status in words in one place; the passage beside the claim on a wide screen and one tap away on a phone; links as lists; dark mode following the system; nothing that needs JavaScript. These follow DESIGN.md and are not up for choice here. What differs: the faces, their size and leading, and the character of the page.

To choose, write the letter in INBOX.md as an owner note, or mark Ask 3 done with the letter. Anything else you notice about the layout is welcome in the same note.
